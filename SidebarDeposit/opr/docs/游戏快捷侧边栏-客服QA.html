游戏快捷侧边栏 · 客服 QAIn-game Quick Sidebar · Customer Service Q&A

版本 / Version：v1.1适用 / For：客服 Customer Service依据 / Based on：PRD、后台操作手册 Admin Manual

适用范围 Scope

H5 和 APP（iOS、安卓）的游戏页，从本功能上线起；原来游戏里的 G 图标按钮升级为新的平台 Logo 按钮（789Bingo / 789games）；旧版本 APP 仍显示 G 图标，更新到最新版本后才有新按钮。
Game pages on H5 and the APP (iOS and Android), from the launch of this feature. The old G button in games is upgraded to the new site logo button (789Bingo / 789games); older app versions still show the G button until the app is updated.

这次改了什么 What changed

玩家在游戏里能看到一个平台 Logo 圆形按钮（789Bingo / 789games），点开是快捷侧边栏：可以回平台首页、充值、刷新余额、看活动、直接换游戏。第一次点退出时侧边栏会自动展开推荐游戏。点「充值」不用退出游戏，会在游戏上方打开充值弹窗，提交后在新页面扫码付款，付完回到原游戏。运营在后台关闭时玩家看不到这个按钮。
Players see a round site logo button (789Bingo / 789games) in the game. Tapping it opens a quick sidebar to go Home, Deposit, Reload the balance, view a promotion, or switch games directly. The first time a player taps Exit, the sidebar opens automatically with recommended games. Tapping Deposit opens a half-screen deposit panel over the game without leaving it; after submitting, the QR payment opens in a new page, and the player returns to the same game after paying. If Operations turns the feature off, the button is not shown.

客服核查步骤 How to check

  1. 功能是否开启：玩家所在站点的后台「活动配置 › 游戏快捷侧边栏管理」，看「启用游戏快捷侧边栏」是否开启。
    Is the feature on: In the Admin of the player's site, 活动配置 › 游戏快捷侧边栏管理 (Promotions › Game Quick Sidebar), check that 启用游戏快捷侧边栏 (Enable sidebar) is on.
  2. 充值订单：按现有充值核查方式查订单状态和到账时间。
    Deposit order: Check the order status and credit time the same way as any other deposit.
  3. 余额：查玩家资金明细，确认换游戏前后余额是否一致。
    Balance: Check the player's fund details to confirm the balance is the same before and after switching games.

问答 Q&A

1我在游戏里充值成功了，为什么游戏里的余额没变？I deposited in the game, but my game balance didn't change. Why?

回复玩家Reply
Your deposit is credited to your account once the payment succeeds. Some games don't refresh the balance on their own—please tap the site logo button and then Reload to see your latest balance. If it still doesn't show, please send us your order number and payment screenshot.
支付成功后充值会到账到您的账户。部分游戏不会自动刷新余额，请点平台 Logo 按钮再点「刷新余额」查看最新余额。如果仍未显示，请把订单号和支付截图发给我们。
客服核查Check
查订单是否成功、是否已到账；已到账则引导玩家点刷新余额。
Check whether the order succeeded and was credited; if credited, guide the player to tap Reload.
转交Escalate
订单成功但未到账，按现有充值问题转交流程处理。
If the order succeeded but was not credited, follow the existing deposit escalation process.

2我点了充值的「提交」，付款页没打开。I tapped Submit to deposit, but the payment page didn't open.

回复玩家Reply
The payment page opens in a new tab, and your browser may have blocked it. Please allow pop-ups for our site and tap Submit again, or deposit from the Wallet page.
付款页面会在新标签页打开，可能被浏览器拦截了。请允许本站弹出窗口后再点一次「提交」，也可以到钱包页充值。
客服核查Check
确认玩家使用的浏览器；查是否生成了未支付订单。多次提交都没付款时，可能已达到未处理订单上限，按现有规则处理。
Confirm which browser the player uses; check whether an unpaid order was created. If the player submitted several times without paying, the unpaid-order limit may have been reached; handle it under the existing rules.
转交Escalate
多名玩家反映同一浏览器打不开时转技术。
If several players report the same browser fails, escalate to Tech.

3我从侧边栏换了游戏，余额会不会少？I switched games from the sidebar. Will I lose any balance?

回复玩家Reply
No. Before you switch, the system finishes the current round and syncs your balance, then takes it to the new game. If the new game can't start, you'll be returned to the previous game with your balance kept.
不会。换游戏前系统会先结算当前局并同步余额，再带到新游戏。如果新游戏打不开，会回到原来的游戏，余额保留。
客服核查Check
查资金明细，核对切换前后的余额。
Check fund details and compare the balance before and after the switch.
转交Escalate
余额对不上时转技术，附玩家账号、两款游戏名称和切换时间。
If the balance doesn't match, escalate to Tech with the player account, both game names and the switch time.

4我一点退出就弹出一个侧边栏，怎么关掉？A sidebar pops up when I tap Exit. How do I close it?

回复玩家Reply
It only appears the first time you tap Exit in each game, to recommend other games. Tap "Keep playing" or "Exit" as usual; tapping Exit again in the same game won't show it. You can also tap the sidebar's collapse button to close both the sidebar and the exit prompt and keep playing.
它只在每款游戏里第一次点退出时出现，用来推荐其他游戏。照常点「继续游戏」或「退出」即可；同一款游戏再点退出不会再出现。也可以点侧边栏的收起按钮，侧边栏和退出提示会一起关闭，继续游戏。
客服核查Check
无需核查。
No check needed.
转交Escalate
同一游戏反复弹出时转技术。
If it appears repeatedly in the same game, escalate to Tech.

5我打开充值时游戏还在跑，错过了下注。The game kept running while I had the deposit panel open, and I missed a bet.

回复玩家Reply
The game keeps running in the background while the deposit panel is open, so you won't lose your game. For live games, we suggest depositing between rounds.
充值面板打开时游戏会在后面继续运行，这样您不会丢失游戏进度。真人游戏建议在两局之间充值。
客服核查Check
查该局投注记录是否正常。
Check that the bet record for that round is normal.
转交Escalate
玩家要求补偿时转运营。
If the player asks for compensation, escalate to Operations.

6我在游戏里首充成功了，为什么没看到首充转盘？I made my first deposit in the game. Why didn't I see the first-deposit spin?

回复玩家Reply
Depositing in the game won't interrupt your play. Your first-deposit rewards and any related steps will show up when you close the game and return to the main page.
在游戏里充值不会打断您的游戏。首充奖励和相关引导会在您关闭游戏、回到平台主页面后显示。
客服核查Check
查订单是否成功、是否为首充；首充转盘是否已在玩家回到平台页面后出现。
Check that the order succeeded and was a first deposit, and whether the spin appeared after the player returned to the main page.
转交Escalate
回到平台页面后仍没有转盘时转运营。
If the spin still doesn't appear on the main page, escalate to Operations.

7我在游戏里点推荐游戏，提示要先认证，游戏还能继续玩吗？I tapped a recommended game and was asked to verify my account. Can I keep playing my current game?

回复玩家Reply
Yes. You stay in your current game and your balance isn't affected. To play the recommended game, please complete account verification first.
可以。您会留在当前游戏，余额不受影响。要玩推荐的游戏，请先完成账户认证。
客服核查Check
按现有 KYC 核查方式查玩家的认证状态。
Check the player's verification status as usual.
转交Escalate
认证已完成仍被拦截时转技术。
If the player is verified but still blocked, escalate to Tech.

8我在 Telegram / Facebook 里玩游戏，充值付完款后停在一个「支付已完成」的页面，回不去游戏。I play inside Telegram / Facebook. After paying, I'm stuck on a "Payment completed" page and can't get back to my game.

回复玩家Reply
Your payment is done. The payment page opened in your phone's browser, so please switch back to Telegram / Facebook (or the installed PWA) — your game is still open there. If the balance hasn't updated, tap the site logo button and then Reload.
您已经支付完成。付款页是在手机浏览器里打开的，请切回 Telegram / Facebook（或已安装的 PWA），游戏还开着。如果余额还没更新，请点平台 Logo 按钮再点「刷新余额」。
客服核查Check
查订单是否成功、是否已到账。提醒玩家不要在手机浏览器里重新登录平台再玩，回到原来的 App 即可。
Check whether the order succeeded and was credited. Remind the player not to log in again in the phone browser; just return to the original app.
转交Escalate
订单成功但未到账，按现有充值问题转交流程处理。
If the order succeeded but was not credited, follow the existing deposit escalation process.

9为什么我的 APP 里还是旧的 G 图标按钮？Why does my app still show the old G button?

回复玩家Reply
Your app version is older. Please update the app to the latest version to use the new site logo button and in-game deposit.
您的 App 版本较旧。请更新到最新版本，就能使用新的平台 Logo 按钮和游戏内充值。
客服核查Check
请玩家提供 App 版本号，对照技术给出的最低支持版本。
Ask for the app version and compare it with the minimum supported version provided by Tech.
转交Escalate
已是最新版本仍显示 G 图标时转技术。
If the app is up to date but still shows the G button, escalate to Tech.

10我在游戏里充值后接着玩，突然弹出要我认证账户，关不掉。After depositing in the game I kept playing, and now a verification pop-up appeared that I can't close.

回复玩家Reply
This is a required account verification under regulations. Please complete it as prompted; your balance is safe and you can continue playing after verification.
这是监管要求的账户认证，请按提示完成。您的余额不受影响，认证后即可继续游戏。
客服核查Check
首充用户玩三方游戏开第 4 局会弹强制 KYC，属现有合规规则，游戏内充值也一样。按现有 KYC 流程跟进。
First-time depositors get a mandatory KYC prompt on their 4th round of third-party games. This is an existing compliance rule and also applies to in-game deposits. Follow the usual KYC process.
转交Escalate
认证已通过仍反复弹出时转技术。
If it keeps appearing after verification is approved, escalate to Tech.

11我点「刷新余额」，却弹出要我去 App 或安装 PWA。I tapped Reload, but it asked me to use the app or install the PWA.

回复玩家Reply
Reloading re-enters the game, so the platform checks your access again. Based on your account status, please continue in our app or PWA as prompted. Your current game and balance are not affected.
刷新余额会重新进入游戏，平台会再次检查进入条件。按您的账户情况，请按提示到 App 或 PWA 继续。您当前的游戏和余额不受影响。
客服核查Check
首充后强制 PWA / APP、余额超过设定金额须在 PWA 投注等现有规则都会拦截；被拦截时不结算、玩家留在当前游戏。
Existing rules such as forced PWA / app after first deposit or PWA-only betting above a set balance can block it; when blocked, nothing is settled and the player stays in the current game.
转交Escalate
玩家不符合这些规则却被拦截时转技术。
If the player doesn't meet these rules but is still blocked, escalate to Tech.

12游戏里充值时为什么看不到每日存送的券？Why can't I see the daily deposit bonus when I deposit in the game?

回复玩家Reply
When the First Deposit bonus is available, it is shown first and the daily deposit bonus is not shown at the same time. After your first deposit, the daily deposit bonus will appear when it's running, and you can choose whether to apply it.
首存活动可参加时会优先显示首存，不会同时显示每日存送。完成首存后，如果每日存送活动正在进行，就会显示出来，您可以自己选择是否申请。
客服核查Check
查玩家是否已首存、后台首存和每日存送活动是否开启。两个活动都没开启时，优惠活动区显示「Keep the game going!」，属正常。
Check whether the player has made a first deposit and whether the First Deposit and daily deposit bonus promotions are on. If neither is on, "Keep the game going!" is shown in the Promotions section instead, which is normal.
转交Escalate
活动已开启、玩家符合条件仍不显示时转技术。
If the promotion is on and the player qualifies but it still doesn't show, escalate to Tech.
