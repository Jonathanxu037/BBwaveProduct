游戏快捷侧边栏 · 客服 QAIn-game Quick Sidebar · Customer Service Q&A

版本 / Version：v1.2适用 / For：客服 Customer Service依据 / Based on：PRD、后台操作手册 Admin Manual

适用范围 Scope

H5 和 APP（iOS、安卓）的游戏页，从本功能上线起；原来游戏里的 G 图标按钮升级为新的悬浮快捷按钮；旧版本 APP 仍显示 G 图标，更新到最新版本后才有新按钮。
Game pages on H5 and the APP (iOS and Android), from the launch of this feature. The old G button in games is upgraded to the new floating quick button; older app versions still show the G button until the app is updated.

这次改了什么 What changed

玩家在游戏里能看到一个悬浮快捷按钮，点开是快捷侧边栏：可以回平台首页、充值、联系客服（CS）、看活动、直接换游戏。在每款游戏第一次点退出时，侧边栏会自动展开推荐游戏。点「充值」不用退出游戏，会在游戏上方打开充值弹窗（占屏幕 2/3），提交后在新页面扫码付款，付完回到原游戏。运营在后台关闭时玩家看不到这个按钮。
Players see a floating quick button in the game. Tapping it opens a quick sidebar to go Home, make a deposit, contact customer service (CS), view a promotion, or switch games directly. The first time a player taps Exit in each game, the sidebar opens automatically with recommended games. Tapping Deposit opens a deposit panel over the game (2/3 of the screen) without leaving it; after submitting, the QR payment opens in a new page, and the player returns to the same game after paying. If Operations turns the feature off, the button is not shown.

客服核查步骤 How to check

  1. 功能是否开启：玩家所在站点的后台「活动配置 › 游戏快捷侧边栏管理」，看「启用游戏快捷侧边栏」是否开启。
    Is the feature on: In the Admin of the player's site, 活动配置 › 游戏快捷侧边栏管理 (Promotions › Game Quick Sidebar), check that 启用游戏快捷侧边栏 (Enable sidebar) is on.
  2. 充值订单：按现有充值核查方式查订单状态和到账时间。
    Deposit order: Check the order status and credit time the same way as any other deposit.
  3. 余额：查玩家资金明细，确认换游戏前后余额是否一致。
    Balance: Check the player's fund details to confirm the balance is the same before and after switching games.

问答 Q&A

1我在游戏里充值成功了，为什么游戏里的余额没变？I deposited in the game, but my game balance didn't change. Why?

回复玩家Reply
Your deposit is credited to your account once the payment succeeds, and your game balance then updates automatically without leaving the game. Some games reload the page automatically to show the new balance. If the current round hasn't finished, or the deposit or chat window is still open, the balance updates after the round ends or the window is closed. If it still doesn't show, please send us your account, order number, deposit time and game name.
支付成功后充值会到账到您的账户，到账后游戏内余额会自动更新，不用退出游戏。部分游戏会自动重新加载页面来显示新余额；如果当前局还没结束，或充值、客服窗口还开着，会在本局结束或关闭窗口后更新。如果仍未显示，请把您的账号、订单号、充值时间和游戏名称发给我们。
客服核查Check
① 查订单是否成功、是否已到账；② 已到账的，确认玩家当前局已结算、充值弹窗和客服窗口都已关闭；③ 都满足后游戏内仍显示旧余额（包括游戏页面已自动刷新过）时，记录账号、订单号、充值时间和游戏名称。
① Check whether the order succeeded and was credited. ② If credited, confirm the current round has been settled and the deposit and chat windows are closed. ③ If all of these are true and the game still shows the old balance (including after the game page reloaded by itself), note the account, order number, deposit time and game name.
转交Escalate
订单成功但未到账，按现有充值问题转交流程处理；符合核查第 ③ 步的情况转技术。
If the order succeeded but was not credited, follow the existing deposit escalation process; in the case described in step ③, escalate to Tech.

18换游戏时提示「免费次数使用中」「余额同步失败」「暂时不可用」等是什么意思？What do messages like "Free Spin in use", "Balance sync failed" or "temporarily unavailable" mean when I switch games?

Your balance is safe in your account.": you were taken to the home page and your balance is kept in your account.
