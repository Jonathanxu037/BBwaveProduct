适用范围 Scope

活动：老用户充值转盘（前台名称 Lucky Spin）。端：所有能参加 Lucky Spin 的端，与现有规则一致。生效：本功能上线后，运营在活动里勾选「原生Gcash」之后，充值成功的订单才适用。
Event: Lucky Spin for existing players (deposit spin event). Platforms: all platforms where Lucky Spin is available, same as today. Effective: applies only to deposits that succeed after this feature goes live AND after Operations adds native GCash to the event.

这次改了什么 What changed

运营现在可以把 GCash 原生支付通道加入 Lucky Spin 活动的充值方式。活动加入该通道后，老用户经该通道充值成功、金额达到活动档位的，按现有规则获得转盘次数；活动没有加入该通道时，经该通道充值不获得转盘次数。档位、次数、奖励和流水要求等其他规则都不变。
Operations can now add the native GCash payment channel to a Lucky Spin event. Once added, existing players whose deposits through this channel are successful and reach the event's amount tier get spins under the existing rules. If the event does not include this channel, deposits through it do not earn spins. All other rules (tiers, number of spins, rewards, wagering requirements) stay the same.
※ 前台可能有多个都叫 GCash 的充值入口，背后是不同的支付通道。只有活动选中的通道才给次数；充值页上带 Lucky Spin 标识的入口才参加活动。
※ The deposit page may show several options named GCash, and each uses a different payment channel. Only channels selected in the event earn spins. Only options showing the Lucky Spin badge on the deposit page are part of the event.

客服核查步骤 How to check

回复玩家前按顺序查，任何一步不满足，按规则不给次数。Check in this order before replying. If any step fails, no spin is given under the rules.

  1. 订单状态：在【财务管理 › 充值记录】查这笔订单是否充值成功。处理中或失败的订单不给次数。
    Order status: In 财务管理 › 充值记录 (Finance › Deposit Records), check whether the order is successful. Processing or failed orders get no spins.
  2. 实际支付通道：在同一条记录里看这笔订单实际走的支付通道（支付名称）。
    Actual channel: In the same record, check which payment channel the order actually used (payment name).
  3. 活动的充值方式：在【活动中心 › 老用户充值转盘】打开玩家充值时正在进行的活动，看「范围配置 › 充值方式」里有没有这笔订单的通道；原生 GCash 对应选项「原生Gcash」。
    Event payment methods: In 活动中心 › 老用户充值转盘 (Activity Center › Lucky Spin for existing players), open the event running at the deposit time and check 范围配置 › 充值方式 (Scope › Payment Methods). Native GCash appears as the option "原生Gcash".
  4. 金额和其他条件：金额是否在活动档位内；充值时间是否在活动时间内；玩家是否满足活动的其他参与条件（如充值次数、是否需要 KYC），以活动配置为准。
    Amount and other conditions: Is the amount within an event tier? Was the deposit made during the event period? Does the player meet the other conditions (such as deposit count and KYC)? Follow the event settings.
  5. 是否已发放：在活动的「活动统计」或玩家资金明细（类型「老用户充值转盘」）查有没有领取记录。
    Already credited? Check the event's 活动统计 (Statistics) tab or the player's 资金明细 (fund details) (type "老用户充值转盘") for a claim record.
  6. 都满足仍没有次数：转技术排查，见「转交与升级」。
    All met but no spin: escalate to Tech. See "Escalation".

问答 Q&A

1我用 GCash 充值了，为什么没有 Lucky Spin？I deposited with GCash. Why didn't I get a Lucky Spin?

回复玩家Reply
Thank you for reaching out. Lucky Spin chances are given only for successful deposits made through a payment option that shows the Lucky Spin badge on the deposit page, and the amount must reach the event's minimum tier. There are several GCash options, and not all of them are part of the event. Please share your order number and deposit time, and we will check it for you.
感谢联系。Lucky Spin 次数只给充值成功、且使用充值页上带 Lucky Spin 标识的支付方式的订单，金额还要达到活动的最低档位。GCash 有多个选项，并不是每个都参加活动。请提供订单号和充值时间，我们帮你查询。
客服核查Check
按「客服核查步骤」第 1～5 步逐项查。
Go through steps 1–5 in "How to check".
转交Escalate
订单成功、通道在活动内、金额在档位内、其他条件都满足仍没有次数时，转技术。
If the order is successful, the channel is in the event, the amount is within a tier, and all other conditions are met but there is still no spin, escalate to Tech.

2哪个 GCash 能参加 Lucky Spin？Which GCash option gives a Lucky Spin?

回复玩家Reply
Please choose the payment option that shows the Lucky Spin badge on the deposit page. The options included can change from event to event, so please check for the badge each time before you deposit.
请选择充值页上带 Lucky Spin 标识的支付方式。参加活动的选项可能随活动变化，每次充值前请先确认有没有这个标识。
客服核查Check
查当前进行中活动的「充值方式」。不要把后台通道名称或编码告诉玩家，只引导玩家看 Lucky Spin 标识。
Check 充值方式 (Payment Methods) of the current event. Do not share internal channel names or codes with players. Only guide them to look for the Lucky Spin badge.

3我之前用 GCash 充值的订单，能补 Lucky Spin 吗？Can I get a Lucky Spin for my earlier GCash deposit?

回复玩家Reply
Lucky Spin chances follow the event rules at the time your deposit was successful. Deposits made before that payment option was added to the event are not eligible.
Lucky Spin 次数按充值成功当时的活动规则判断。该支付方式加入活动之前的充值订单不参加活动。
客服核查Check
对比订单的充值成功时间和活动加入原生Gcash 的时间。加入后才充值成功的订单如果没有次数，按第 1 条处理。
Compare the order's success time with the time native GCash was added to the event. If the order succeeded after it was added and has no spin, follow Q1.

4我的充值还在处理中 / 失败了，会给 Lucky Spin 吗？My deposit is still processing or failed. Will I get a Lucky Spin?

回复玩家Reply
Lucky Spin chances are given only after a deposit is successful. If your deposit is still processing, please wait for it to complete. Failed deposits are not eligible.
Lucky Spin 次数只在充值成功后给。充值还在处理中的，请等处理完成；充值失败的订单不参加活动。
客服核查Check
查订单状态。订单后来变为成功仍没有次数时，按第 1 条处理。
Check the order status. If it later becomes successful and still has no spin, follow Q1.

5充多少钱才有 Lucky Spin？能转几次？How much do I need to deposit, and how many spins can I get?

回复玩家Reply
The minimum deposit and the number of spins depend on the current event. Please check the Lucky Spin details shown on the event and deposit pages.
最低充值金额和转盘次数按当前活动规定。请以活动页和充值页上显示的 Lucky Spin 说明为准。
客服核查Check
金额档位在活动「奖励配置」里，首次和非首次分别配置。回复时不要说具体数字，以页面显示为准。
Tiers are set in the event's 奖励配置 (Reward Settings), separately for first-time and non-first-time. Do not quote exact numbers. Refer players to what the page shows.

6我是新玩家，为什么参加不了 Lucky Spin？I'm a new player. Why can't I join Lucky Spin?

回复玩家Reply
Lucky Spin is for players who already have a deposit record. New players can check the events available for new players on the promotions page.
Lucky Spin 面向已有充值记录的玩家。新玩家可以在活动页查看面向新玩家的活动。
客服核查Check
看活动「范围配置」的人群范围和用户充值次数条件（现有活动为充值次数 ≥ 1），以活动配置为准。
Check the audience and deposit count condition under 范围配置 (Scope) (current events use deposit count ≥ 1). Follow the event settings.

7Lucky Spin 的奖金能马上提现吗？Can I withdraw my Lucky Spin reward right away?

回复玩家Reply
Lucky Spin rewards have a wagering (turnover) requirement set by the event. You can withdraw after you complete it. We can help you check the requirement for your reward.
Lucky Spin 奖金有活动规定的流水要求，完成后才能提现。我们可以帮你查这笔奖金的流水要求。
客服核查Check
看活动「奖励提现」：首次和非首次的流水倍数，以及流水是否包含本金、是否包含奖金。
Check 奖励提现 (Reward Withdrawal) in the event: the turnover multiplier for first-time and non-first-time, and whether turnover includes the deposit and the bonus.

8在哪里看 Lucky Spin 的奖金？Where can I see my Lucky Spin reward?

回复玩家Reply
You can find the reward in your account's transaction records. If you can't find it, please share your account and the time you spun, and we will check it for you.
奖金可以在你账户的交易记录里查看。找不到的话，请提供账号和转盘时间，我们帮你查询。
客服核查Check
在玩家资金明细里查类型「老用户充值转盘」的记录，或在活动「活动统计」里查领取金额和领取时间。
Look for type "老用户充值转盘" in the player's 资金明细 (fund details), or check the claim amount and time in the event's 活动统计 (Statistics) tab.

9GCash 选项上有 Lucky Spin 标识，我也充值成功了，还是没有次数。The GCash option showed the Lucky Spin badge and my deposit was successful, but I still didn't get a spin.

回复玩家Reply
Sorry for the inconvenience. Please send us your order number, deposit time, amount, and a screenshot of the payment option you used. We will pass it to the relevant team to check.
抱歉给你带来不便。请提供订单号、充值时间、金额和所用支付方式的截图，我们转给相关团队核查。
客服核查Check
先按核查步骤查一遍。前台有标识但活动没选这个通道，或者活动选了但前台没标识，属于配置和展示不一致，按「转交与升级」处理。不要向玩家承诺补发。
Run the checks first. If the page shows the badge but the event does not include the channel (or the reverse), it is a mismatch between settings and display. Follow "Escalation". Do not promise any compensation.
