版本 / Version：v1.2适用 / For：客服 Customer Service依据 / Based on：PRD、后台操作手册 Admin Manual

适用范围 Scope

VIP 会员体系 v1.2（VIP中心、升级奖金、月度奖金、保级与降级、生日奖金），适用 H5、iOS、安卓新版本 App，从运营开启 VIP 体系起生效。全部时间按 GMT+8（菲律宾时间）。具体奖金以 VIP中心等级表为准，门槛以后台【VIP会员等级配置】为准，运营可调整。
VIP program v1.2 (VIP Center, Upgrade Bonus, Monthly Bonus, level retention and drops, Birthday Bonus) on H5 and the latest iOS and Android apps, effective once Operations turns the VIP program on. All times are GMT+8 (Philippine time). Exact bonus amounts follow the level table in VIP Center; requirements follow 【VIP会员等级配置】 in the back office. Operations may adjust both.

这次改了什么 What changed

新增 VIP 等级（默认 25 个：VIP1–VIP24、SUPER VIP，运营可在最高一级之上新增等级或删除最高一级），未达到 VIP1 的玩家为 VIP0（前台显示「会员」）。累计有效投注和累计充值同时达到要求即实时升级，每个等级首次到达时自动发一次升级奖金；每月有效投注达到当前等级的保级投注即保级成功，次月 1 日 13:00 自动发月度奖金，未达到次月降 1 级；生日奖金金额在生日所在月份 1 日结算后按等级确定，生日当天起可在 VIP中心领取。原会员等级（Level）不再使用，等级中心（Level Center）改为 VIP中心；旧版 VIP 体系（VIP0–VIP8、成长值、下注限额与返水，以及旧版 VIP活动）被完全替换，不再存在。上线时只按累计有效投注和累计充值定级，已达到的等级不发升级奖金；原 Level 和旧版 VIP 未到账的奖励一律作废。
We added VIP levels (25 by default: VIP1–VIP24, SUPER VIP; Operations can add a level above the top or remove the top level); players below VIP1 are VIP0 in the back office (shown as "Member" in the app). When both total valid bets and total deposits meet a level's requirements, the player levels up instantly and gets that level's Upgrade Bonus automatically, once per level. If monthly valid bets reach the current level's retention bet, the player keeps the level and receives the Monthly Bonus automatically at 13:00 on the 1st of next month; otherwise the level drops by 1 next month. The Birthday Bonus amount is set by the level after settlement on the 1st of the birthday month, and players can claim it in VIP Center from their birthday. Member Levels are no longer used, and Level Center has become VIP Center; the old VIP program (VIP0–VIP8, growth points, bet limits and rebates, and the old VIP promotion) has been fully replaced and no longer exists. At launch, levels are set only from total valid bets and total deposits, with no Upgrade Bonus for levels already reached; unpaid rewards from the old Levels and old VIP program are all voided.

  1. 查等级和进度：本期后台没有 VIP会员数据查询页（Q23 已定不补）。玩家问自己的等级和进度时，先请玩家看 App 里的 VIP中心；需要核实当前等级、历史最高等级、累计有效投注、累计充值、本月保级状态时，转运营由技术查库。
    Level and progress: The back office has no VIP member lookup page in this release (Q23: not added). Ask the player to check VIP Center in the app first; if the current level, highest level, total valid bets, total deposits or retention status must be verified, pass it to Operations for a database check by Tech.
  2. 查等级变动：本期后台没有等级变动记录查询页（Q23 已定不补）。每月结算造成的保级、降级、恢复，在【用户管理 › VIP会员等级配置 › VIP月度结算记录】对应月份点「明细」按会员账号查；实时升级、上线定级、KYC 定级等其他变动，记下会员账号和时间，转运营由技术查库。
    Level changes: No level change lookup page in this release (Q23: not added). For monthly retention, drops and restores, open the month in 【用户管理 › VIP会员等级配置 › VIP月度结算记录】 (VIP Monthly Settlement Records), click Details and search the account. For other changes (instant level-ups, launch leveling, KYC leveling, etc.), note the account and time and pass it to Operations for a database check by Tech.
  3. 查奖金：【用户管理 › VIP会员等级配置 › 活动奖励统计】输入会员账号、选好申请领取时间后点「搜索」，看奖励类型、奖励金额、状态（状态下方小字为原因）、审核发放时间。
    Bonuses: 【用户管理 › VIP会员等级配置 › 活动奖励统计】 (Reward Statistics): enter the account, set the date range and click Search; check reward type, amount, status (the reason is shown in small text under the status) and credit time.
  4. 查月度结算：【用户管理 › VIP会员等级配置 › VIP月度结算记录】看该月结算状态（已完成 / 结算中 / 结算失败）和人数；点「明细」按会员账号查考核等级、当月有效投注、保级投注、结算结果、结算后等级、月度奖金和奖金状态。月度奖金的处理（审核、放行、补发）看【活动奖励统计】奖励类型「月度奖金」。
    Monthly settlement: In 【用户管理 › VIP会员等级配置 › VIP月度结算记录】 (VIP Monthly Settlement Records), check the month's status (Completed / In Progress / Failed) and counts; click Details and search the account for the assessed level, monthly valid bets, retention bet, result, level after settlement, Monthly Bonus and its status. For Monthly Bonus handling (review, release, reissue), see 【活动奖励统计】 with reward type "Monthly Bonus".
  5. 查参数：【VIP会员等级配置 › VIP会员等级奖励配置】看顶部「VIP领取奖励」（即 VIP 体系开关）、「VIP会员等级奖励规则」里的 KYC 限制、强制审核是否开启，流水倍数和生日奖金有效期。
    Settings: 【VIP会员等级奖励配置】 (VIP Reward Settings): whether "VIP领取奖励" (the VIP program switch), the KYC requirement and mandatory review are on, plus the turnover multiple and Birthday Bonus validity.

6降级后我的累计还够，为什么不能马上升回去？After the drop my totals are still enough. Why can't I go back up right away?

回复玩家Reply
After a drop, your level comes back one step at a time: if your valid bets this month reach the retention bet of your current level, you get that level's Monthly Bonus as usual and move up 1 level next month, up to the highest level you have reached. For example, if you were VIP10 in October and missed VIP10's retention bet of ₱3M, you dropped to VIP9 on November 1; if you then reach VIP9's retention bet of ₱1M in November, you get VIP9's ₱200 Monthly Bonus on December 1 and return to VIP10. If your totals reach a level higher than your highest level so far, you level up instantly.
降级后按月逐级恢复：当月有效投注达到你当前等级的保级投注，照常获得当前等级的月度奖金，次月恢复 1 级，最多恢复到你到达过的最高等级。例如 10 月是 VIP10、没达到 VIP10 的保级投注 ₱3M，11 月 1 日降为 VIP9；11 月达到 VIP9 的保级投注 ₱1M，12 月 1 日会收到 VIP9 月度奖金 ₱200，并回到 VIP10。累计达到比你以前最高等级更高的等级要求时，会立即升级。
