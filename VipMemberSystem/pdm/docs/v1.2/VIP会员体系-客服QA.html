这次改了什么 What changed

  • 等级：默认 25 级（VIP1–VIP24、SUPER VIP），未达到 VIP1 的为 VIP0（前台、后台都显示「VIP0」）；运营可在最高一级之上新增等级，或删除最高一级。
    Levels: 25 by default (VIP1–VIP24, SUPER VIP); players below VIP1 are VIP0 (shown as "VIP0" in both the app and the back office). Operations can add a level above the top or remove the top level.
  • 升级：累计有效投注和累计充值同时达到要求即实时升级；每个等级首次到达时发一次升级奖金。
    Level-up: instant when both total valid bets and total deposits meet the requirements; each level's Upgrade Bonus is paid once, the first time the level is reached.
  • 口径：累计充值沿用平台现有累计充值口径；有效投注为平台自算，计入所有游戏，同账号或关联账号同局对押、同场对立盘口的注单不计入（规则 N3「累计充值」、N4「有效投注」）。
    Counting: total deposits follow the platform's existing deposit total; valid bets are calculated by the platform across all games, excluding opposite bets on the same round or opposite sides of the same event by the same or linked accounts (rules N3, N4).
  • 保级与降级：每月初结算（时间由后台配置，对玩家只说「每月初」）。当月有效投注达到哪一级的保级投注，就发哪一级的月度奖金（最高到历史最高等级）；未达到当前等级保级投注的，次月降 1 级；降级后达到更高等级保级投注的，次月直接恢复到该级，可一次恢复多级（用户 10-29 定）。
    Retention and drops: settled at the start of each month (time set in the back office; tell players only "at the start of each month"). The player gets the Monthly Bonus of the highest level whose retention bet the month's valid bets reached (up to the highest level ever reached). Missing the current level's retention bet drops the level by 1 next month; a dropped player who reaches a higher level's retention bet goes straight back to that level next month, several levels at once if reached.
  • 生日奖金：金额按生日所在月份结算后的等级确定，生日当天起可领；只认第一次 KYC 审核通过时的出生日期，改生日的当年和次年没有，同一 KYC 身份的多个账号每年只有 1 个账号有（规则 N21「资格与生成」）。
    Birthday Bonus: the amount follows the level after settlement in the birthday month; claimable from the birthday. Only the date of birth from the first approved KYC counts; none in the year the birthday is changed or the year after; only one account per KYC identity gets it each year (rule N21).
  • 领取：三种奖金默认由玩家登录后在 VIP中心手动领取，不设审核；共用「领奖有效期」，默认 15 天（含升级当天、结算完成当天或生日当天），过期不能领（用户 10-29 定）。
    Claiming: by default, all three bonuses are claimed manually by the player in VIP Center after logging in, with no review step. They share one claim validity, 15 days by default (counting the day of upgrade, settlement or the birthday); expired bonuses cannot be claimed.
  • 旧体系停用：原会员等级（Level）停用，等级中心（Level Center）直接替换为 VIP中心，老版本 App 的入口也进入 VIP中心；旧版 VIP（VIP0–VIP8、成长值、下注限额、返水、旧版 VIP活动）全部取消。
    Old programs removed: Member Levels are no longer used and Level Center is replaced by VIP Center, including the entry in older app versions; the old VIP program (VIP0–VIP8, growth points, bet limits, rebates and the old VIP promotion) is fully removed.
  • 上线：只按累计有效投注和累计充值定级，已达到的等级不发升级奖金；原 Level 和旧版 VIP 未到账的奖励全部作废。
    Launch: levels are set only from total valid bets and total deposits, with no Upgrade Bonus for levels already reached; all unpaid rewards from the old Levels and old VIP program are voided.

    Level changes: For monthly retention, drops and restores, open the month in 【用户管理 › VIP会员等级配置 › VIP月度结算记录】 (VIP Monthly Settlement Records), click Details and search the account. Other changes (instant level-ups, launch leveling, KYC leveling, etc.) have no lookup page; note the account and time and pass it to Operations for a database check by Tech.
  3. 奖金：【用户管理 › VIP会员等级配置 › 活动奖励统计】输入会员账号，点「搜索」（申请领取时间可不填），看奖励类型、奖励金额、状态（原因或截止时间在「说明」列）、申请领取时间（未领取显示「-」）、发放时间（实际到账时间）。
    Bonuses: In 【用户管理 › VIP会员等级配置 › 活动奖励统计】 (Reward Statistics), enter the account and click Search (the date range can be left empty). Check reward type, amount, status (reason or deadline in the Notes column), claim time ("-" if not claimed) and credit time.
  4. 月度结算：【VIP月度结算记录】看该月状态（已完成 / 部分完成 / 结算中 / 结算失败，只在后台显示，前台不显示；「部分完成」= 部分玩家结算失败，待补结算）和人数。点「明细」按会员账号查考核等级、当月有效投注、保级投注、结算结果、结算后等级、月度工资、生日奖金和到账核算。发放状态以及放行、作废、补发都在【活动奖励统计】（明细里点「查看记录」可直接跳转）。

Click Details and search the account for the assessed level, monthly valid bets, retention bet, result, level after settlement, Monthly Salary, Birthday Bonus and credited summary. Payout status, release, void and reissue are all in 【活动奖励统计】 ("View Records" in the details jumps there).

4为什么我的 VIP 等级降了？降级了为什么还有月度奖金？Why did my VIP level drop? Why did I still get a Monthly Bonus?

回复玩家Reply
To keep your level, your valid bets in each calendar month (GMT+8) must reach your level's retention bet. If they don't, your level drops by 1 the next month (at most 1 level per month; VIP1 drops to VIP0). If your bets reached a lower level's retention bet, you still get that level's Monthly Bonus. For example, a VIP5 player who bets ₱30,000 in a month (VIP2's retention bet is ₱20,000) drops to VIP4 and gets VIP2's ₱12 Monthly Bonus.
每个自然月（GMT+8）的有效投注要达到当前等级的保级投注才能保级；未达到的，次月降 1 级（每月最多降 1 级，VIP1 降为 VIP0）。当月投注达到较低等级保级投注的，仍发那一级的月度奖金。例如 VIP5 当月投注 ₱30,000（VIP2 保级投注 ₱20,000），次月降为 VIP4，并可领取 VIP2 月度奖金 ₱12。

5我降级了，怎么回到原来的等级？累计够了为什么不能马上升回去？My level dropped. How do I get it back? My totals are enough, why not right away?

回复玩家Reply
After a drop, your level comes back at monthly settlement, not right away. Next month you return to the highest level whose retention bet your valid bets reached this month (up to your highest level so far), and you get that level's Monthly Bonus. You can get several levels back at once. For example, if your highest level is VIP5 and you are now VIP2: bet ₱80,000 this month (VIP4's retention bet) and you return to VIP4 after monthly settlement at the start of next month, with VIP4's ₱28 Monthly Bonus; bet ₱100,000 or more (VIP5's retention bet) and you return to VIP5 with VIP5's ₱38 Monthly Bonus. If your totals reach a level above your highest level so far, you level up right away. You can see this month's progress on your current level card in VIP Center.
降级后不会因累计够了立即升回，而是在每月初结算时恢复：当月有效投注达到哪一级的保级投注，次月就回到那一级（最多到你到达过的最高等级），并获得那一级的月度奖金，可一次恢复多级。例如历史最高 VIP5、现在是 VIP2：当月有效投注 ₱80,000（VIP4 的保级投注），次月初结算后回到 VIP4，可领 VIP4 月度奖金 ₱28；当月 ₱100,000 及以上（VIP5 的保级投注），回到 VIP5，可领 VIP5 月度奖金 ₱38。累计达到比你以前最高等级更高的等级要求时，会立即升级。本月进度可在 VIP中心当前等级卡上查看。
