这次改了什么 What changed

玩家完成 KYC 后加入 VIP 会员体系。达到 VIP 等级的要求就立即升级，首次到达某个 VIP 等级时发放该等级的升级奖励（每级只发一次）。每个自然月要达到当前等级的保级要求：达到的，次月 1 日结算后保持等级并获得保级奖励；没达到的，不发保级奖励，次月降 1 级；降级后达到恢复条件可逐月恢复。每年生日可领取生日奖励。升级奖励、保级奖励自动到账，生日奖励在等级中心领取。还没有 VIP 等级的玩家显示为「普通用户」，前台不显示会员等级（LEVEL）。VIP 体系上线时，玩家按当时的历史累计数据直接定级，已到达的等级不补发升级奖励。本期不含 VIP 实物奖品。
Players join the VIP Program after completing KYC. A player moves up as soon as they meet a VIP level's requirement, and the Upgrade Bonus of a level is given the first time they reach it (once per level). Each calendar month, the player must meet the retention requirement of their current level. If they do, they keep the level and get the Retention Bonus after the settlement on the 1st of the next month. If not, no Retention Bonus is given and they drop 1 level next month; after a drop, they can move back up month by month by meeting the recovery condition. A Birthday Bonus can be claimed once a year. Upgrade and Retention Bonuses are credited automatically, and the Birthday Bonus is claimed in Level Center. Players without a VIP level are shown as a regular member, and member levels (LEVEL) are not shown in the app. When the VIP Program launches, each player is placed at a VIP level based on their history at that time; levels already reached do not get Upgrade Bonuses afterwards. VIP physical prizes are not included in this version.

回复玩家前按顺序查。后台时间均为 GMT+8，注意和玩家所说时间对齐。Check in this order before replying. Admin times are GMT+8; make sure they match the time the player mentions.

  1. 端、开关和 KYC：先问清是 H5 还是 App、App 是否最新版本；再看「用户管理 › VIP特权 › VIP特权 › VIP全局参数」的「VIP 体系状态」是否为开启；再看玩家 KYC 是否已通过（未完成 KYC 的玩家不参与 VIP 体系）。
    Platform, switch and KYC: First ask whether the player uses H5 or the App, and whether the App is the latest version. Then check whether 「VIP 体系状态」 (VIP Program status) is on in 用户管理 › VIP特权 › VIP特权 › VIP全局参数 (User Management › VIP Privileges › VIP Privileges › VIP Global Settings). Then check whether the player's KYC is verified (players without completed KYC are not in the VIP Program).
  2. 后台模式：在「VIP特权 › VIP等级配置」看 VIP0 是否勾选「沿用普通会员级别」（勾选：VIP 玩家不再领普通会员级别奖励；不勾选：普通会员和 VIP 两套独立，两边奖励各自可领）和计算方式。两种情况下前台等级中心都只有一页，不显示会员等级（LEVEL）。
    Admin mode: In VIP特权 › VIP等级配置 (VIP Privileges › VIP Level Settings), check whether VIP0 has 「沿用普通会员级别」 (share member levels) ticked (ticked: VIP players no longer get member-level rewards; not ticked: member levels and VIP levels are independent, and rewards on both sides can be received), and check the calculation method. In both cases, Level Center has one page and does not show member levels (LEVEL).
  3. 当前等级和最高等级：在「VIP特权 › VIP会员数据」按会员账号查询，看当前等级和历史最高等级。两者不同说明玩家降过级；降级玩家的「本月保级要求」下方有恢复目标和所需投注。
    Current and highest level: In VIP特权 › VIP会员数据 (VIP Privileges › VIP Member Data), search by player account and check the current level and the highest level. If they differ, the player has dropped a level before; for such players, the recovery target and the bets needed are shown under the monthly retention requirement.
  4. 升级门槛：在同一页看累计充值、累计有效投注（计算方式为成长值时看成长值），对照「VIP等级配置」中下一级的门槛，看哪一项还没达到。
    Level-up requirements: On the same page, check total deposits and total valid bets (or the growth value if the calculation method is growth value), and compare them with the requirements of the next level in VIP等级配置 (VIP Level Settings). See which one is not reached yet.
  5. 保级进度：在同一页看本月有效投注、本月保级要求和本月保级进度。
    Retention progress: On the same page, check this month's valid bets, this month's retention requirement and retention progress.
  6. 升降级经过：在「VIP特权 › 等级变动记录」按会员账号查升级、保级成功、降级、恢复、退出 VIP 段、存量初始化的时间和触发原因。
    Level history: In VIP特权 › 等级变动记录 (VIP Privileges › Level Change Records), search by player account for the time and trigger of each level-up, retention, drop, recovery, exit from the VIP levels and launch placement (存量初始化).
  7. 奖励发放：VIP 奖励在「VIP特权 › 会员福利记录」按会员名和福利类别（VIP 升级奖励 / VIP 保级奖励 / VIP 生日奖励）查状态和到账时间；普通会员级别的奖励在「会员等级 › 活动奖励统计」查。
    Rewards: For VIP rewards, search by player name and reward type (VIP upgrade / VIP retention / VIP birthday) in VIP特权 › 会员福利记录 (VIP Privileges › Member Benefit Records) for the status and credit time. Member-level rewards are in 会员等级 › 活动奖励统计 (Member Levels › Reward Statistics).
  8. 账号状态：在会员列表的「编辑用户」看账号状态（正常、冻结、停用、禁止投注等）、风控原因和是否绑定手机号。自我排除不是单独的账号状态：账号为停用或冻结、且风控原因为 Self-Exclusion 的，就是自我排除。冻结、停用、禁止投注、自我排除和风险分 70 分及以上的，奖励暂停发放，也不发 VIP 短信；风险分 40–69 的奖励和短信照常；未绑定手机号的奖励照常、不发短信。
    Account status: In the member list (编辑用户, Edit User), check the account status (normal, frozen, disabled, betting banned, etc.), the risk reason, and whether a mobile number is linked. Self-exclusion is not a separate status: an account that is disabled or frozen with the risk reason Self-Exclusion is self-excluded. For frozen, disabled, betting-banned or self-excluded accounts, and accounts with a risk score of 70 or above, rewards are put on hold and no VIP SMS is sent. Accounts with a risk score of 40–69 get rewards and SMS as usual. Accounts without a linked mobile number get rewards as usual but no SMS.
  9. 月度结算：保级相关问题，在「VIP特权 › 月度结算记录」看上月结算是否「完成」。
    Monthly settlement: For retention questions, check in VIP特权 › 月度结算记录 (VIP Privileges › Monthly Settlement Records) whether last month's settlement is complete.

If your totals reach the requirement of a level higher than your highest VIP level, you move up right away.

8保级怎么算？什么时候结算？How does VIP retention work, and when is it settled?

回复玩家Reply
Retention only counts your valid bets in each calendar month (from the 1st at 00:00 to the last day at 23:59:59, GMT+8). Deposits are not counted. The requirement depends on your current level; if you moved up during the month, the new level's requirement applies. Your current level card in Level Center shows how much more you need this month. Results are settled on the 1st of the next month.
保级只看每个自然月（1 日 00:00 到最后一天 23:59:59，GMT+8）的有效投注，不看充值。要求按你的当前等级而定；当月升过级的，按升级后的等级要求。等级中心当前等级卡上会显示本月还差多少。次月 1 日结算。
客服核查Check
按核查步骤第 5 步查。只算当月结算的注单，按结算时间归月；月末 23:59:59 后结算的注单算下个月。当月升过级、达到新等级要求的，照常保级并发保级奖励。计算方式为成长值时按当月新增成长值考核，前台只显示进度条，回复不提成长值。
Follow step 5. Only bets settled in the month count, by settlement time; bets settled after 23:59:59 on the last day count toward the next month.
