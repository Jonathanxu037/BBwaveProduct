适用范围 Scope

VIP 会员体系 v1.3：VIP中心、升级奖金、每月奖金、等级保持与降级、生日奖金，以及升级弹窗、待领取奖金通知、短信、站内信和 APP 推送。适用 H5（PC 浏览器打开的就是 H5 版本，功能相同）和 iOS、安卓 App（「VIP会员系统」第一次开启前，App 须更新到含 VIP中心的版本）。后台「VIP会员系统」开启时前台显示 VIP中心，关闭时 VIP中心整体隐藏。时间均为 GMT+8（菲律宾时间）。奖金金额以 VIP中心等级表为准，门槛以后台【VIP会员等级配置】为准，运营可调整。
VIP program v1.3: VIP Center, Upgrade Bonus, Monthly Bonus, keeping and dropping levels, Birthday Bonus, plus the level-up pop-up, the bonus reminder pop-up, SMS, inbox messages and app push. Works on H5 (a PC browser opens the same H5 version) and the iOS and Android apps (before the VIP program is first switched on, the app must be updated to a version with VIP Center). VIP Center is shown while the "VIP会员系统" (VIP program) switch in the back office is on, and is hidden completely while it is off. All times are GMT+8 (Philippine time). Bonus amounts follow the level table in VIP Center; requirements follow 【VIP会员等级配置】 in the back office. Operations may adjust both.
叫法：前台、后台都叫「每月奖金」；玩家看到的「每月奖金投注」= 后台等级表的「每月奖金标准」。后台结算结果写「保级成功」「未保级与降级」。回复玩家一律说「每月奖金」「每月奖金投注」。
Names: both the app and the back office say "Monthly Bonus". The "Monthly Bonus bet" players see is "每月奖金标准" (Monthly Bonus requirement) in the back-office level table. Settlement results in the back office say "保级成功" (kept) and "未保级与降级" (not kept / dropped). Always say "Monthly Bonus" and "Monthly Bonus bet" to players.
回复玩家时：结算时间只说「每月初」，不说具体日期和时间；不提奖金的流水要求（玩家问能否提现时按第 14 条答复）。前台在结算期间照常显示结算前的数据，不显示任何结算提示（规则 N12「统计周期与结算时刻」⑤、Q59「前台不显示结算」、Q60「前台不显示流水」）。
When replying to players: say only "at the start of each month" for settlement, never a specific date or time; do not mention bonus wagering requirements (for withdrawal questions, see item 14). During settlement the app keeps showing the data from before settlement, with no settlement notice (rule N12 ⑤ settlement period; Q59 no settlement notice in the app; Q60 no wagering info in the app).

规则要点 Key rules

参见 PRD 规则 N52：累计开关生效与存量截止日期。

  • 等级：默认 25 级（VIP1–VIP24、SUPER VIP），未达到 VIP1 的为 VIP0（前台、后台都显示「VIP0」）；运营可在最高一级之上新增等级，或删除最高一级。
    Levels: 25 by default (VIP1–VIP24, SUPER VIP); players below VIP1 are VIP0 (shown as "VIP0" in both the app and the back office). Operations can add a level above the top or remove the top level.
  • 升级：累计有效投注和累计充值同时达到要求即实时升级；每个等级首次到达时发一次升级奖金。
    Level-up: instant when both total valid bets and total deposits meet the requirements; each level's Upgrade Bonus is paid once, the first time the level is reached.
  • 口径：累计充值沿用平台现有累计充值口径；有效投注为平台自算，计入所有游戏，同账号或关联账号同局对押、同场对立盘口的注单，以及单注赔率低于平台指定值的体育注单不计入（规则 N3「累计充值」、N4「有效投注」）。
    Counting: total deposits follow the platform's existing deposit total; valid bets are calculated by the platform across all games, excluding opposite bets on the same round or opposite sides of the same event by the same or linked accounts, and Sports bets with odds below the platform’s specified minimum (rules N3 total deposits, N4 valid bets).
  • 每月奖金与降级：每月初结算（时间由后台配置，对玩家只说「每月初」）。当月有效投注达到哪一级的每月奖金投注，就发哪一级的每月奖金（最高到历史最高等级）；未达到当前等级每月奖金投注的，次月降 1 级；降级后达到更高等级每月奖金投注的，次月直接恢复到该级，可一次恢复多级。
    Monthly Bonus and drops: settled at the start of each month (time set in the back office; tell players only "at the start of each month"). The player gets the Monthly Bonus of the highest level whose Monthly Bonus bet the month's valid bets reached (up to the highest level ever reached). Missing the current level's Monthly Bonus bet drops the level by 1 next month; a dropped player who reaches a higher level's Monthly Bonus bet goes straight back to that level next month, several levels at once if reached.
  • 开始结算的时间：「VIP会员系统」第一次开启后满 28 天，之后的第一个每月初结算才开始评定等级和发每月奖金；在此之前的每月初不评定等级（等级不变）、没有每月奖金，但当月生日的玩家照常有生日奖金（生日不早于开启日期的，见第 35 条）。再次开启不重新计 28 天，下一个每月初照常结算。
    When settlement starts: monthly assessment starts only at the first start-of-month settlement at least 28 days after the VIP program is first switched on. Before that, start-of-month settlements do not assess levels (levels stay the same) and pay no Monthly Bonus, but players with a birthday in that month still get the Birthday Bonus (if the birthday is not earlier than the switch-on date; see item 35). Switching the program on again does not restart the 28 days; the next start-of-month settlement runs as usual.
  • 通知语言：VIP 短信、站内信、推送只发英文，日期写法如「Oct 27」。
    Notification language: VIP SMS, inbox messages and push are sent in English only, with dates such as "Oct 27".
  • 生日奖金：金额按生日所在月份结算后的等级确定；从生日和奖金生成日中较晚的一天起可领，有效期也从这一天起算；只认第一次 KYC 审核通过时的出生日期，改生日的当年和次年没有，同一 KYC 身份的多个账号每年只有 1 个账号有（规则 N21「资格与生成」、N22「领取与有效期」）。
    Birthday Bonus: the amount follows the level after settlement in the birthday month. It is claimable from the later of the birthday and the day the bonus is created, and its validity counts from that day. Only the date of birth from the first approved KYC counts; none in the year the birthday is changed or the year after; only one account per KYC identity gets it each year (rules N21 eligibility and creation, N22 claiming and validity).
  • 领取：三种奖金默认由玩家登录后手动领取，不设审核；各有「领奖有效期」，默认 15 天（含起算当天），过期不能领；某类开启「可累计领取」（默认不开启）后，新生成的奖金不过期，已有记录保留原截止日期。通知按每笔记录判断：合并记录中有会过期的奖金时写最早截止日期，全部不过期才省略日期。回复玩家时说「在奖金旁显示的截止日期前领取」（奖金旁没有截止日期的，说「这笔奖金不会过期，随时可以领取」），不说具体天数。升级奖金在升级弹窗点「领取」，或在 VIP中心对应等级卡「查看详情」里领；每月奖金、生日奖金在当前等级卡「查看详情」里领。运营可把某类奖金设为自动领取（生成即到账；生日奖金在可领取当天到账）。
    Claiming: by default, all three bonuses are claimed manually by the player after logging in, with no review step. Each type has its own claim validity, 15 days by default (counting the start day); expired bonuses cannot be claimed. When accumulated claiming is turned on for a type (off by default), newly created bonuses do not expire; existing records keep their original deadline. A combined reminder, inbox message or push shows the earliest deadline among expiring records, and omits it only when all records are non-expiring. When replying, say "claim it before the deadline shown next to it" (if no deadline is shown, say "this bonus does not expire and can be claimed any time"); never quote a number of days. The Upgrade Bonus is claimed with "Claim" on the level-up pop-up or under "View Details" on that level card in VIP Center; Monthly and Birthday Bonuses under "View Details" on the current level card. Operations can set a bonus type to auto-claim (credited as soon as it is created; the Birthday Bonus on its claim start day).
  • 旧体系停用：原会员等级（Level）停用，等级中心（Level Center）直接替换为 VIP中心，原 Level 和 VIP 特权功能全部作废，App 须更新到含 VIP中心的版本；旧版 VIP（VIP0–VIP8、成长值、下注限额、返水、旧版 VIP活动）全部取消；原 Level 30+ 权益（VIP 专属礼物、快速提款通道、VIP 专属客服、VIP 专属群）取消，见第 34 条。
    Old programs removed: Member Levels are no longer used and Level Center is replaced by VIP Center; the old Level and VIP privilege features are all retired, and the app must be updated to a version with VIP Center; the old VIP program (VIP0–VIP8, growth points, bet limits, rebates and the old VIP promotion) is fully removed; the old Level 30+ benefits (VIP exclusive gifts, fast withdrawal channel, VIP exclusive support, VIP exclusive group) are discontinued, see item 34.
  • 上线：只按累计有效投注和累计充值定级，已达到的等级不发升级奖金；原 Level 和旧版 VIP 未到账的奖励全部作废。
    Launch: levels are set only from total valid bets and total deposits, with no Upgrade Bonus for levels already reached; all unpaid rewards from the old Levels and old VIP program are voided.

升级弹窗、通知和其他要点 Level-up pop-up, notifications and other key points

参见 PRD 第 6 章：通知发送时实际长度校验及超长处理。

  • 升级弹窗：升级时只弹平台现有的升级弹窗（画面、音效沿用现网），等级显示 VIPn，没有「查看权益」按钮。有待领取的升级奖金时弹窗只有「领取」按钮，领取后关闭；没领就退出的，下次进入平台补弹（见第 24 条）。升级奖金和生日奖金都是手动领取、且同时弹出时，同一弹窗分两行显示升级奖金和生日奖金，点「领取」一起领取。
    Level-up pop-up: on level-up, only the platform's existing level-up pop-up appears (visuals and sound as on the platform today), showing VIPn, with no "View Benefits" button. With an Upgrade Bonus to claim, the pop-up has only a "Claim" button and closes after claiming; if the player leaves without claiming, it shows again on the next visit to the platform (see item 24). When the Upgrade Bonus and the Birthday Bonus are both manual claim and pop up together, the same pop-up shows them on two rows, and one tap on "Claim" claims both.
  • 可累计领取：三类奖金分别设置，默认关闭。每月、生日奖金关闭后，正常结算生成新一笔时，较早未领取的同类奖金作废；升级奖金及等级调整补偿不适用，未过期的可合计领取。开启只影响之后新生成的奖金，存量保留原截止日期；关闭时存量处理及例外见第 31 条。
    Accumulated claiming: Each bonus type has its own accumulated-claiming setting, off by default. For Monthly and Birthday Bonuses with this setting off, a new bonus from normal settlement voids earlier unclaimed records of the same type. Upgrade Bonuses and Level Adjustment Compensation are exempt; unexpired records can be claimed together. Turning it on affects only newly created bonuses; existing records keep their deadline. See item 31 for switch-off handling and exceptions.
  • VIP会员系统关闭：前台 VIP中心整体隐藏；关闭时玩家未领取的奖金由运营统一处理，按记录分别到账、作废或保留原状态（见第 32 条）。运营修改等级表前须先关闭，所以关闭可能只是短暂维护。
    VIP program switched off: VIP Center is hidden completely; players' unclaimed bonuses are handled by Operations at switch-off: records are credited, voided or left unchanged according to their status and batch (see item 32). Operations must switch it off before changing the level table, so a switch-off may be only short maintenance.
  • 通知：奖金直接到账（自动领取、运营放行或补发、关闭系统或关闭可累计领取时手动发放）时发「已到账」站内信、推送和到账短信，玩家自己点领取到账时不发；一次升多级只发 1 条站内信和推送；每条通知可设是否遵守免打扰，站内信、推送可分别关闭（见第 7、33 条）。
    Notifications: when a bonus is credited directly (auto-claim, release or reissue by Operations, or manual payout when the VIP program or accumulated claiming is switched off), a "credited" inbox message, push and credited SMS are sent; none when the player claims it themselves. A multi-level upgrade sends only 1 inbox message and 1 push. Each notification can be set to follow quiet hours, and inbox messages and push can be turned off separately (see items 7 and 33).
  • 其他页面：个人中心、聊天室头像下的「Lv n」改为「VIPn」，头像框按段位分 8 档；「Level Center」入口改名「VIP Center」；聊天室领奖播报显示奖金名称（见第 17、29 条）。
    Other pages: "Lv n" under avatars in the account page and chat room becomes "VIPn"; avatar frames follow 8 tiers; the "Level Center" entry is renamed "VIP Center"; bonus announcements in the chat room show the bonus name (see items 17 and 29).

客服核查步骤 How to check

  1. 等级和进度：先请玩家看 App 里的 VIP中心。后台本期没有 VIP 会员查询页（Q23 后台查询页：不补）；当前等级、历史最高等级、累计有效投注、累计充值在【用户管理 › 会员】会员详情的 VIP 只读项查看（用户 2026-11-02）；本月每月奖金进度需要核实时，转运营由技术查询。
    Level and progress: Ask the player to check VIP Center in the app first. The back office has no VIP member lookup page in this release (Q23 back-office lookup pages: not added); the current level, highest level, total valid bets and total deposits are shown as read-only VIP fields in the member details under 【用户管理 › 会员】; to verify this month's Monthly Bonus progress, pass it to Operations for a check by Tech.
  2. 等级变动：月度结算造成的保级、降级、恢复，在【用户管理 › VIP会员等级配置 › VIP月度结算记录】对应月份点「明细」，按会员账号查。其他变动（实时升级、上线定级、KYC 定级、新增或删除等级后的重算、再次开启后的核算）没有查询页，记下会员账号和时间，转运营由技术查询。
    Level changes: For monthly keeps, drops and restores, open the month in 【用户管理 › VIP会员等级配置 › VIP月度结算记录】 (VIP Monthly Settlement Records), click Details and search the account. Other changes (instant level-ups, launch leveling, KYC leveling, recalculation after adding or deleting levels, recalculation at switch-on again) have no lookup page; note the account and time and pass it to Operations for a check by Tech.
  3. 奖金：【用户管理 › VIP会员等级配置 › 活动奖励统计】输入会员账号，点「搜索」（生成时间可不填），看奖励类型、奖励金额、状态（共 7 种：已到账、待领取、暂停发放（风控）、已过期、已作废、不发放、发放失败；原因或截止时间在「说明」列）、申请领取时间（未领取显示「-」）、发放时间（实际到账时间）。
    Bonuses: In 【用户管理 › VIP会员等级配置 › 活动奖励统计】 (Reward Statistics), enter the account and click Search (the creation-time range can be left empty). Check reward type, amount, status (7 in total: Credited, Pending Claim, Paused (risk), Expired, Voided, Not Paid, Failed; reason or deadline in the Notes column), claim time ("-" if not claimed) and credit time.
  4. 月度结算：【VIP月度结算记录】看该月状态（已完成 / 部分完成 / 结算中 / 结算失败 / 已中止 / 未结算（系统关闭），只在后台显示，前台不显示；「部分完成」= 部分玩家结算失败，待补结算；「已中止」= 结算进行中「VIP会员系统」被紧急关闭，已结算玩家的等级和奖金照常有效，其余玩家待运营「继续结算」）和人数。点「明细」按会员账号查考核等级、当月有效投注、每月奖金标准、结算结果、结算后等级、每月奖金、生日奖金和到账核算。发放状态以及放行、作废、补发都在【活动奖励统计】（明细里点「查看记录」可直接跳转）。
    Monthly settlement: In 【VIP月度结算记录】, check the month's status (Completed / Partially completed / In Progress / Failed / Aborted / Not settled (program off), back office only; "Partially completed" means some players failed and await re-settlement; "Aborted" means "VIP会员系统" was switched off in an emergency during settlement: players already settled keep their levels and bonuses, and the rest wait for Operations to click "继续结算" (Continue Settlement)) and counts. Click Details and search the account for the assessed level, monthly valid bets, Monthly Bonus requirement, result, level after settlement, Monthly Bonus, Birthday Bonus and credited summary. Payout status, release, void and reissue are all in 【活动奖励统计】 ("View Records" in the details jumps there).
  5. 参数：【VIP会员等级配置】页签（现网「会员等级配置」「会员等级奖励配置」两个页签合并而成）：顶部「VIP会员系统」（VIP 体系开关，关闭时前台隐藏 VIP中心）；规则区的 KYC 限制、三种奖金的领取方式（自动 / 手动，默认手动）和各自的「可累计领取」（默认不开启）、各自的领奖有效期（默认 15 天；开启可累计领取的类型置灰、不过期）、月度结算时间（只供内部核查，不告诉玩家）、流水倍数、通知配置（免打扰时段、短信和推送的开关与内容）；下方等级表（门槛、每月奖金标准、升级奖金、每月奖金、生日奖金）。
    Settings: In the 【VIP会员等级配置】 tab (merges the platform's current "Level Settings" and "Level Reward Settings" tabs): at the top, "VIP会员系统" (the VIP program switch; VIP Center is hidden while it is off); in the rules area, the KYC requirement, each bonus's claim method (auto / manual, manual by default) and its "可累计领取" (accumulated claiming, off by default), its own claim validity (15 days by default; greyed out and no expiry for types with accumulated claiming on), monthly settlement time (internal only, never tell players), turnover multiple, and notification settings (quiet hours, on/off and content of SMS and push); below, the level table (requirements, Monthly Bonus requirement, Upgrade, Monthly and Birthday Bonuses).
  6. 操作记录：放行、作废、补发、开关切换、配置修改等操作记录在【日志管理 › 操作日志】查询（VIP 配置页本身没有「操作日志」入口）。本期不设第二人审批；等级表确认后保存配置、重新开启时生效，其他操作按各自规则执行。
    Operation records: releases, voids, reissues, switch changes and setting changes are looked up in the platform's existing user operation log module (the VIP settings page itself has no "Operation Log" entry). There is no second-approver step in this release. Level-table changes are saved on confirmation and take effect when the VIP program is switched on again; other actions follow their own rules.
  7. 通知发送记录：短信按平台现有短信发送记录查；站内信和推送（含因免打扰顺延的推送）是否发出、何时发出，在平台现有推送记录的查询入口（菜单路径上线前由技术核对，没有的统一转技术查询）查；平台查不到的，记下会员账号、奖金类型和时间，转技术查询（用户 2026-11-02）。
    Notification records: check SMS in the platform's existing SMS send records; check whether and when inbox messages and push (including push delayed by quiet hours) were sent in the platform's existing push record lookup. If they cannot be found there, note the account, bonus type and time and pass it to Tech.
