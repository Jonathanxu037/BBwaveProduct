适用范围 Scope

客服后台所有会话列表和会话详情。上线时间另行通知；玩家端没有变化。
All conversation lists and conversation details in the CS console. Release date to be announced; nothing changes on the player side.

这次改了什么 What changed

每条会话用户名后显示身份和等级（VIP、LV、会员、游客）；排序方式新增「User level: Highest first」并设为默认，列表默认按等级从高到低排序，同一等级先来的在前。
Each conversation shows the player's tier and level (VIP, LV, Member, Guest) after the name. A new sort option, "User level: Highest first", is the default: the list is sorted from the highest level down, and within the same level, earlier handoffs come first.

客服核查步骤 How to check

  1. 看身份和等级：会话列表用户名后的标签；「游客」先核实玩家账号。
    Check tier and level: the tag after the name in any conversation list; for "Guest", verify the player's account first.

适用范围 Scope

客服对话窗（首页悬浮客服按钮进入，或游戏页内打开）里的快捷问题「我的充值没到账，怎么办？」，H5、Android、iOS、PWA 都适用。上线时间另行通知。
The quick question "My deposit is missing. What do I do?" in the chat window (opened from the floating support button on the home page, or inside a game). Applies to H5, Android, iOS and PWA. Release date to be announced.

这次改了什么 What changed

玩家点「我的充值没到账，怎么办？」后，看到的是最近交易卡片和「查看订单」按钮，不再是文字回复；打开订单页后可返回对话。其他快捷问题不变。客服后台会话里只显示玩家点的问题。
When a player taps "My deposit is missing. What do I do?", instead of a text reply they see their latest transaction card with a "View Orders" button that opens the Orders page; going back returns to the chat. Other quick questions are unchanged. In the CS console the conversation shows only the question the player tapped.

客服核查步骤 How to check

  1. 核查充值订单：请玩家在订单页点一行复制订单号发过来，再按现有流程在管理后台查询充值订单状态。
    Check the deposit order: ask the player to tap a row on the Orders page to copy the order number and send it, then check the deposit status in the admin console as usual.
