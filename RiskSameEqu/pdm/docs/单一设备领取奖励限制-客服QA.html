版本 / Version：v1.0适用 / For：客服 Customer Service依据 / Based on：PRD、后台操作手册 Admin Manual

适用范围 Scope

所有活动，H5、PWA、iOS、Android 均适用。运营按活动设置同一设备、同一网络（IP）最多领取几次，限制周期可选活动期间、每天、7 天、15 天或一个月（自然月）。未设置的活动不受限制。单个账号的领取次数沿用各活动自身规则。
All promotions on H5, PWA, iOS and Android. For each promotion, Operations sets how many times one device or one network (IP) can claim it, counted over the whole promotion, per day, per 7 or 15 days, or per calendar month. Promotions without these settings are not limited. Each account's own limit follows the promotion's existing rules.

提示编号对照 Message codes

被限制时，玩家看到统一提示「Reward claim limit [编号]」，不发奖励、不扣参与次数。客服按编号判断原因。充值页的优惠被限制时不可选，下方显示同样的提示。自动发放的奖励被限制时不发放，没有提示。
When blocked, the player sees "Reward claim limit [code]". No reward is given and no chances are used. Use the code to identify the reason. On the deposit page, a blocked offer cannot be selected and shows the same message. Auto-credited rewards that are blocked are not credited, with no message.

客服核查步骤 How to check

  1. 问清情况：玩家在哪个活动、提示里的编号、这台设备或这个网络（如家里 Wi-Fi）是否有其他账号（含家人）用过。
    Ask: which promotion, the code in the message, and whether other accounts (including family members) have used this device or network (such as home Wi-Fi).
  2. 判断是否误伤：玩家只用自己的账号，或与家人共用设备、网络且都是正常用户时，转运营核实。
    Judge: if the player only uses their own account, or family members share the device or network and are genuine players, escalate to Operations.
  3. 运营处理：核实后把该会员导入这个活动的「领取限制白名单」并保存活动（只对这个活动有效）；也可以调大次数。
    Operations: after checking, Operations imports the member into that promotion's "Claim Limit Whitelist" and saves the promotion (it only applies to that promotion). Operations may also raise the limit.

问答 Q&A

1提示「Reward claim limit [L01]」或「[L02]」，我从没领过，为什么？It says "Reward claim limit [L01]" or "[L02]", but I never claimed it. Why?

回复玩家Reply
This promotion can only be claimed a limited number of times per device and per network. Another account on your device or network may have already claimed it. You can still join other promotions. If no one else uses this device or network, please tell us your account and we will check for you.
该活动在同一设备、同一网络上的领取次数有限，您设备或网络上的其他账号可能已经领取过。其他活动仍可正常参加。如果没有其他人使用这台设备或这个网络，请告诉我们您的账号，我们帮您核查。
客服核查Check
确认账号、活动名称和编号（L01 设备、L02 网络），问清设备、网络是否与他人共用。
Confirm the account, promotion name and code (L01 device, L02 network), and whether the device or network is shared.
转交Escalate
玩家坚持没有他人使用，或确属家人共用 → 转运营核实是否加白名单。
If the player insists no one else uses it, or family members genuinely share it → escalate to Operations to decide on the whitelist.

2提示「Reward claim limit [L03]」怎么办？What should I do when I see "Reward claim limit [L03]"?

回复玩家Reply
We could not verify your device or network just now. Your chances have not been used. Please refresh the page or reopen the app and try again.
刚才没能核验您的设备或网络，您的领取次数没有被扣除。请刷新页面或重新打开 App 后再试。
客服核查Check
问清使用的端（H5、App）和浏览器。
Ask which platform (H5, app) and browser they use.
转交Escalate
多次重试仍失败 → 转技术。
Still failing after several tries → escalate to Tech.

3充值页的优惠为什么选不了？Why can't I select the offer on the deposit page?

回复玩家Reply
This offer has reached its claim limit on your device or network. You can still deposit without the offer, or choose another available offer.
这个优惠在您的设备或网络上的领取次数已达上限。您仍可以不选优惠充值，或选择其他可用的优惠。
客服核查Check
确认优惠名称和优惠下方提示里的编号。
Confirm the offer name and the code shown under it.
转交Escalate
玩家认为被误伤 → 转运营核实。
If the player believes it is a mistake → escalate to Operations.

4这次为什么没收到自动发放的奖励（如登录彩金）？Why didn't I receive the auto-credited reward (such as the login bonus) this time?

回复玩家Reply
Each promotion can only be credited a limited number of times per device and per network. This reward may have already been credited to another account on your device or network. Please tell us your account and we will check for you.
每个活动在同一设备、同一网络上的发放次数有限，这次奖励可能已发给您设备或网络上的其他账号。请告诉我们您的账号，我们帮您核查。
客服核查Check
确认账号、活动名称和应发放日期，问清设备、网络是否与他人共用。
Confirm the account, promotion name and expected date, and whether the device or network is shared.
转交Escalate
转运营核实；确属误伤时，运营可把会员导入该活动的白名单。
Escalate to Operations; if it is a mistake, Operations can import the member into that promotion's whitelist.

内部说明 Internal notes

  • （内部说明，不对玩家解释）本版同一设备换浏览器、在网页和 App 之间切换、清除缓存或重装 App 后，可能被识别成新设备，属已知情况，后续对接三方风控服务后优化。玩家询问设备如何识别时，转运营。
    (Internal only, do not explain to players) In this version, switching browsers, switching between the website and the app, clearing cache or reinstalling the app may make the same device look like a new one. This is known and will be improved after a third-party risk control service is connected. Escalate questions about how devices are identified to Operations.
  • （内部说明）充值到账时优惠因达到上限未发放时，系统不另行通知玩家；玩家询问时，说明该优惠在其设备或网络上的领取次数已达上限，充值金额已正常到账。
    (Internal only) When a deposit is credited but the offer is not given because the limit was reached, the player is not notified. If asked, explain that the offer has reached its claim limit on their device or network, and that the deposit has been credited normally.
