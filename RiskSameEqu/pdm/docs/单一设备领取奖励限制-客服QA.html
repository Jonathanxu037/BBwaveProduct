单一设备领取奖励限制 · 客服 QAPromotion Claim Limits · Customer Service Q&A

版本 / Version：v1.0适用 / For：客服 Customer Service依据 / Based on：PRD、后台操作手册 Admin Manual

适用范围 Scope

所有活动，H5、PWA、iOS、Android 均适用。运营按活动设置同一设备、同一网络（IP）、同一账号最多领取几次，限制周期可选活动期间、每天、7 天、15 天或一个月（自然月）。未设置的活动不受限制。
All promotions on H5, PWA, iOS and Android. For each promotion, Operations sets how many times one device, one network (IP) or one account can claim it, counted over the whole promotion, per day, per 7 or 15 days, or per calendar month. Promotions without these settings are not limited.

这次改了什么 What changed

超过次数时不发奖励、不扣参与次数，并提示：账号达到上限「Claim limit reached for this promotion.」，设备达到上限「Claim limit reached on this device.」，网络达到上限「Claim limit reached on this network.」。充值页的优惠达到上限时不可选，玩家仍可不选优惠充值。自动发放的奖励达到上限时不发放，无提示。不同活动分别计算。
Beyond the limit, no reward is given and no chances are used. The player sees "Claim limit reached for this promotion." (account), "Claim limit reached on this device." (device) or "Claim limit reached on this network." (network). On the deposit page, an offer that has reached its limit cannot be selected, but the player can still deposit without it. Auto-credited rewards beyond the limit are not credited, with no message. Each promotion is counted separately.

客服核查步骤 How to check

  1. 问清情况：玩家在哪个活动、看到哪句提示、这台设备或这个网络（如家里 Wi-Fi）是否有其他账号（含家人）用过。
    Ask: which promotion, which message, and whether other accounts (including family members) have used this device or network (such as home Wi-Fi).
  2. 判断是否误伤：玩家只用自己的账号，或与家人共用设备、网络且都是正常用户时，转运营核实。
    Judge: if the player only uses their own account, or family members share the device or network and are genuine players, escalate to Operations.
  3. 运营处理：核实后把该会员加入这个活动的「领取限制白名单」，立即生效（只对这个活动有效，账号自己的次数上限照常）；也可以调大次数。
    Operations: after checking, Operations adds the member to that promotion's "Claim Limit Whitelist", which takes effect immediately. It only applies to that promotion, and the account's own limit still applies. Operations may also raise the limit.

问答 Q&A

1为什么提示这台设备或这个网络领取次数已达上限？我从没领过。Why does it say the limit is reached on this device or network? I never claimed it.

回复玩家Reply
Each promotion can only be claimed a limited number of times per device and per network. Another account on this device or network may have already claimed it. You can still join other promotions. If no one else uses this device or network, please tell us your account and we will check for you.
每个活动在同一设备、同一网络上的领取次数有限，这台设备或这个网络上的其他账号可能已经领取过。其他活动仍可正常参加。如果没有其他人使用这台设备或这个网络，请告诉我们您的账号，我们帮您核查。
客服核查Check
确认玩家账号、活动名称和提示内容，问清设备、网络是否与他人共用。
Confirm the account, promotion name and message, and whether the device or network is shared.
转交Escalate
玩家坚持没有他人使用，或确属家人共用 → 转运营核实是否加白名单。
If the player insists no one else uses it, or family members genuinely share it → escalate to Operations to decide on the whitelist.

2提示「Claim limit reached for this promotion.」，为什么不能再领了？It says "Claim limit reached for this promotion." Why can't I claim again?

回复玩家Reply
You have reached the maximum number of claims for this promotion. Please check the promotion rules, and feel free to join our other promotions.
您在这个活动的领取次数已达上限。请查看活动规则，也欢迎参加其他活动。
客服核查Check
确认活动名称；这是账号自己的次数上限，不涉及其他账号。
Confirm the promotion name. This is the account's own limit and does not involve other accounts.
转交Escalate
玩家认为次数不对 → 转运营核实。
If the player thinks the count is wrong → escalate to Operations.

3提示「Verifying your eligibility. Please try again later.」怎么办？What should I do when I see "Verifying your eligibility. Please try again later."?

回复玩家Reply
We could not verify your device just now. Your chances have not been used. Please refresh the page or reopen the app and try again.
刚才没能核验您的设备，您的领取次数没有被扣除。请刷新页面或重新打开 App 后再试。
客服核查Check
问清使用的端（H5、App）和浏览器。
Ask which platform (H5, app) and browser they use.
转交Escalate
多次重试仍失败 → 转技术。
Still failing after several tries → escalate to Tech.

4充值页的优惠为什么选不了？Why can't I select the offer on the deposit page?

回复玩家Reply
This offer has reached its claim limit for your account, device or network. You can still deposit without the offer, or choose another available offer.
这个优惠在您的账号、设备或网络上的领取次数已达上限。您仍可以不选优惠充值，或选择其他可用的优惠。
客服核查Check
确认优惠名称和优惠下方的提示内容。
Confirm the offer name and the message shown under it.
转交Escalate
玩家认为被误伤 → 转运营核实。
If the player believes it is a mistake → escalate to Operations.

5这次为什么没收到自动发放的奖励（如登录彩金）？Why didn't I receive the auto-credited reward (such as the login bonus) this time?

回复玩家Reply
Each promotion can only be credited a limited number of times per account, device and network. This reward may have already been credited to another account on your device or network, or your account has reached its limit. Please tell us your account and we will check for you.
每个活动在同一账号、设备、网络上的发放次数有限。这次奖励可能已发给您设备或网络上的其他账号，或您的账号已达到上限。请告诉我们您的账号，我们帮您核查。
客服核查Check
确认账号、活动名称和应发放日期，问清设备、网络是否与他人共用。
Confirm the account, promotion name and expected date, and whether the device or network is shared.
转交Escalate
转运营核实；确属误伤时，运营可把会员加入该活动的白名单。
Escalate to Operations; if it is a mistake, Operations can add the member to that promotion's whitelist.

内部说明 Internal notes

  • （内部说明，不对玩家解释）本版同一设备换浏览器、在网页和 App 之间切换、清除缓存或重装 App 后，可能被识别成新设备，属已知情况，后续对接三方风控服务后优化。玩家询问设备如何识别时，转运营。
    (Internal only, do not explain to players) In this version, switching browsers, switching between the website and the app, clearing cache or reinstalling the app may make the same device look like a new one. This is known and will be improved after a third-party risk control service is connected. Escalate questions about how devices are identified to Operations.
  • （内部说明）充值到账时优惠因达到上限未发放时，系统不另行通知玩家；玩家询问时，说明该优惠在其账号、设备或网络上的领取次数已达上限，充值金额已正常到账。
    (Internal only) When a deposit is credited but the offer is not given because the limit was reached, the player is not notified. If asked, explain that the offer has reached its claim limit for their account, device or network, and that the deposit has been credited normally.
