适用范围 Scope

789Bingo、789Games 两个站【活动中心】下设置了领取限制的活动，H5、PC、PWA、iOS、Android 均适用。运营在后台按活动设置同一设备、同一网络（IP）的限制，限制周期可选活动期间、每天、7 天、15 天或一个月（自然月）：
① 每个账号只能领一次的活动（绑定手机号码、KYC 认证送彩金、新用户首充、导入用户登录送彩金（789Games 叫登录送彩金活动（白名单）））：限制同一设备、同一网络最多有几次领取。
② 一个账号可以领多次的活动（如签到、充值优惠、转盘、返水、Crown Rush（皇冠冲榜））：限制同一设备、同一网络能领取的账号数，每个周期内只有最先领取的几个账号（数量由运营设置）能领，之后的账号都领不了；能领的账号自己按活动原规则继续领。
未设置的活动不受限制。单个账号的领取次数沿用各活动自身规则。同一设备、同一网络的领取限制统一按上述设置判定（邀请好友的邀请资格规则、完成任务砸金蛋的每天名额按活动自身规则），被限制时玩家都看到带编号的提示。
Promotions in the Promotions center of both 789Bingo and 789Games that have claim limits, on H5, PC, PWA, iOS and Android. For each promotion, Operations sets limits for one device or one network (IP) in the admin, counted over the whole promotion, per day, per 7 or 15 days, or per calendar month:
① Promotions each account can claim only once (Bind Mobile Number, KYC bonus, New User First Top-Up, the whitelist login bonus): a maximum number of claims per device and per network.
② Promotions an account can claim many times (such as check-in, deposit offers, spins, rebates and Crown Rush): a limited number of accounts per device and per network. In each period, only the first few accounts that claim (the number is set by Operations) can claim; later accounts cannot. Those accounts keep claiming under the promotion's own rules.
Promotions without these settings are not limited. Each account's own limit follows the promotion's existing rules. Device and network claim limits all follow these settings (the referral eligibility rules of Invite Friends and the daily quota of the Golden Egg task promotion follow their own rules), and a blocked player always sees a coded message.

提示编号对照 Message codes

被限制时，玩家看到统一提示「Reward claim limit [编号]」，不发奖励，不扣参与次数。客服按编号判断原因。充值页可选的优惠（充值活动、新用户充值活动计划）被限制时不可选，下方显示同样的提示。自动发放的奖励（含每日首充、新用户首充）被限制时不发放，也没有提示、站内信或推送。老用户充值转盘、返现充值转盘被限制时，充值后不给转盘次数、不弹出转盘，也没有提示。
When blocked, the player sees "Reward claim limit [code]". No reward is given and no chances are used. Use the code to identify the reason. On the deposit page, a blocked selectable offer (deposit offers, new user deposit plans) cannot be selected and shows the same message. Auto-credited rewards (including Daily First Top-Up and New User First Top-Up) that are blocked are not credited, with no message, inbox message or push notification. When the Old User Deposit Spin or the Cashback Deposit Spin is blocked, no spin is given or shown after the deposit, with no message.

客服核查步骤 How to check

  1. 问清情况：玩家参加的是哪个活动、提示里的编号是什么、这台设备或这个网络（如家里的 Wi-Fi）上是否有其他账号（含家人）用过。
    Ask: which promotion, the code in the message, and whether other accounts (including family members) have used this device or network (such as home Wi-Fi).
  2. 判断是否误伤：玩家只用自己的账号，或与家人共用设备、网络且都是正常玩家时，转运营核实。
    Judge: if the player only uses their own account, or family members share the device or network and are genuine players, escalate to Operations.
  3. 运营处理：核实后，运营在后台把该会员导入这个活动（分期的活动为这一期）的领取限制白名单，只对这个活动有效，所有活动都可以这样处理。线下投注站代办的会员，确认后直接加白名单。
    Operations: after checking, Operations imports the member into the Claim Limit Whitelist of this promotion (or of this round, for promotions with rounds) in the admin. It only applies to that promotion, and every promotion supports it. Members served by an offline betting shop are added to the whitelist directly once confirmed.

6家人在这台手机上签到（或领其他可以多次领的活动）后，我的账号在同一台手机上一直领不了，为什么？After a family member checked in (or claimed another repeatable promotion) on this phone, my account cannot claim on the same phone. Why?

回复玩家Reply
For this promotion, only a limited number of accounts can claim on each device or network during each period, and other accounts on your device or network have already reached that limit. You can still join other promotions. If no one else uses this device or network, please tell us your account and we will check for you.
该活动在每个周期内，每台设备、每个网络能领取的账号数有限，您设备或网络上的其他账号已经领满。其他活动仍可正常参加。如果没有其他人使用这台设备或这个网络，请告诉我们您的账号，我们帮您核查。
客服核查Check
一个账号可以多次领取的活动（如签到、充值优惠、转盘、返水、Crown Rush（皇冠冲榜）），运营勾选限制并设置账号数 N 后，每个周期内按首次领取成功的先后，最先在该设备（或网络）领取的 N 个账号才能领；这 N 个已满时，其他账号领取该活动的任何奖励都提示 L01（同设备）或 L02（同网络）；签到被拦截时不记签到。周期为「每天」「一个月」的，下一个周期开始后重新按领取先后计算；周期为「7 天」「15 天」的，看往前 7 / 15 天内在这台设备（或网络）领过的账号，按这段时间内首次领取成功的先后取前 N 个，玩家在前 N 个里或这段时间内领过的账号不到 N 个才能领，例如选 7 天、1 个账号，家人最后一次领取是 11 月 4 日，玩家 11 月 10 日仍领不了，11 月 11 日可以领；周期为「活动期间」的整个活动期间累计。运营在活动进行中开启限制或调小账号数后，只有本周期内最先领取的 N 个账号还能领，之前领过的其他账号也会被限制。确认账号、活动名称、编号，问清设备、网络是否与家人共用。
For promotions an account can claim many times (such as check-in, deposit offers, spins, rebates and Crown Rush), once Operations enables the limit and sets the number of accounts N, only the first N accounts that successfully claim on a device (or network) in each period, in order of their first claim, can claim; once these N are filled, any claim by other accounts shows L01 (device) or L02 (network), and a blocked check-in is not recorded. For "per day" and "per month", the count restarts by claim order when the next period starts; for "7 days" and "15 days", look at the accounts that claimed on that device (or network) in the past 7 or 15 days and take the first N by their first successful claim in that window; the player can claim only if the account is among those N or fewer than N accounts claimed in that window, for example with 7 days and 1 account, if a family member last claimed on 4 November, the player still cannot claim on 10 November but can on 11 November; if the period is "Whole promotion", it counts over the whole promotion. If Operations enables the limit or lowers the number of accounts while the promotion is running, only the first N accounts that claimed in the current period can still claim, and other accounts that claimed before are also limited. Confirm the account, promotion name and code, and whether the device or network is shared with family members.

确属家人共用、都是正常玩家 → 转运营核实，按「客服核查步骤」第 3 步处理。
If family members genuinely share the device and are genuine players → escalate to Operations, handled as in step 3 of "How to check".

If asked, explain that the offer has reached its claim limit on their device or network, and that the deposit has been credited normally.
