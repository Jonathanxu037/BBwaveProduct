版本 / Version：v1.0适用 / For：客服 Customer Service依据 / Based on：v1.0 PRD

适用范围 Scope

789Bingo 游戏内浮动侧栏上的 Quest 按钮，只对应圣诞这一期 Crown Rush 活动。适用 H5（网页版）、iOS App、安卓 App；PC、老版本 App 和安卓原生老包没有这个按钮。H5 在圣诞活动预热开始前上线，App 在包含此功能的新版本上线后才有。
The Quest button on the floating in-game sidebar of 789Bingo, for the Christmas Crown Rush event only. Available on H5, iOS app and Android app; not available on PC, older app versions or the old native Android app. H5 goes live before the Christmas warm-up starts; the apps show it only after updating to the version that includes it.

这次改了什么 What changed

玩家在游戏中展开侧栏（点 G 图标），会在 Reload 和 G 图标之间看到 Quest 按钮。点它会像点 Home 一样先离开游戏，再打开圣诞 Crown Rush 活动页。圣诞 Crown Rush 处于预热中、进行中或领奖期时显示，下架（领奖期结束）后自动隐藏。活动规则和奖励没有变化。
When players open the in-game sidebar (tap the G icon), they see a Quest button between Reload and the G icon. Tapping it leaves the game just like Home, then opens the Christmas Crown Rush event page. It shows while the event is in its warm-up, live or claim window, and is hidden once the event is taken down (after the claim window). Event rules and rewards are unchanged.

客服核查步骤 How to check

  1. 问端和版本：H5、iOS 还是安卓 App；App 是否已更新到最新版本。
    Ask device and version: H5, iOS or Android app; whether the app is up to date.
  2. 看活动阶段：圣诞 Crown Rush 是否在预热、进行中或领奖期间；下架后按钮不显示。
    Check the event period: whether the Christmas Crown Rush is in its warm-up, live or claim window; the button is hidden after take-down.
  3. 问是否展开侧栏：侧栏收起时只看到 G 图标。
    Ask whether the sidebar is open: when collapsed, only the G icon shows.

2我点了这个按钮，为什么游戏退出了？I tapped the button, why did my game close?

回复玩家Reply
The Quest button works like the Home button: it leaves the game first, then opens the event page. Your bets are settled under our standard rules, the same as when you tap Home. You can open the game again from the lobby at any time.
Quest 按钮和 Home 一样，会先离开游戏再打开活动页。注单按平台标准规则结算，和点 Home 相同。你可以随时从大厅重新进入游戏。
客服核查Check
玩家质疑注单或余额时，查投注记录核对该局结算结果。
If the player questions a bet or balance, check the betting history.
转交Escalate
结算结果与点 Home 不一致或余额异常：转技术。
If settlement differs from Home or the balance looks wrong: escalate to Tech.

3我在游戏里找不到 Quest 按钮。I can't find the Quest button in the game.

回复玩家Reply
Please tap the G icon in the game to open the sidebar; the Quest button is next to it. If you are using the app, please update it to the latest version. The button is not available on PC, and it only shows while the Christmas Crown Rush event is in its warm-up, live or claim window.
请在游戏里点 G 图标展开侧栏，Quest 按钮就在它旁边。用 App 的请更新到最新版本。PC 没有这个按钮；按钮只在圣诞 Crown Rush 处于预热中、进行中或领奖期时显示。
客服核查Check
按「客服核查步骤」1–3 查。
Follow steps 1–3 of "How to check".

H5 或最新版 App、活动期间、侧栏已展开仍没有按钮：转技术，附端、App 版本、游戏名称、时间（GMT+8）和截图。
On H5 or the latest app, during the event, sidebar open and still no button: escalate to Tech with device, app version, game name, time (GMT+8) and a screenshot.
