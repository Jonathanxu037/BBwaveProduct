用户分享追踪 · 客服 QAShare Task for Big Spin Winners · Customer Service Q&A

版本 / Version：v1.0适用 / For：客服 Customer Service依据 / Based on：PRD、后台操作手册 Admin Manual

适用范围 Scope

789Bingo、789Games 两站（H5、App）。适用于受限用户：本功能上线后，在已开启分享任务的新用户转盘活动中，单次中奖金额满足触发条件的新用户（判断方式和触发金额按活动配置，默认大于等于 ₱789；第 3 次转盘的大礼包也计入）。
789Bingo and 789Games (H5 and App). Applies to restricted players: after this feature goes live, new users whose single prize in a New User Spin Wheel promotion with the share task turned on meets the trigger condition (set per promotion: the comparison is "equal to or more than" or "exactly equal to" the trigger amount, and the trigger amount is configurable, default "₱789 or more"; the third-spin bonus pack also counts).

这次改了什么 What changed

① 受限用户进入游戏前必须绑定谷歌邮箱（用 Google 账号授权；没有 Google 账号的，先去 Google 官方注册）。② 受限用户提交提款时，未绑定谷歌邮箱的先绑定，再完成分享任务（在弹窗里点「去分享」，按弹窗显示的次数完成），完成一次永久有效。本功能上线前已有的用户、不满足触发条件的玩家不受影响；现有提款审核规则不变。
① Restricted players must link a Google email before playing (by signing in with Google; players without a Google account create one on Google first). ② When a restricted player submits a withdrawal, they must first link a Google email if they have not, then complete the share task in the pop-up (tap "Share" the number of times shown). Once completed, it is never required again. Players who joined before this feature went live and players who do not meet the trigger condition are not affected. Existing withdrawal review rules stay the same.

客服核查步骤 How to check

  1. 是否受限：在后台「活动中心 › 新用户转盘活动 › 分享用户记录」按会员账号搜索；查得到记录，且「绑定状态」为「未绑定」或「任务状态」为「未完成」时，玩家当前受限；显示「-」「已绑定」「已完成」「手动解除」的项目已不再限制。可同时查看「活动编号」和「中奖金额」。
    Restricted or not: In 活动中心 › 新用户转盘活动 › 分享用户记录 (Promotions › New User Spin › Share Records), search by account. The player is currently restricted only if a record is found and 绑定状态 (Link Status) shows 未绑定 (Not linked) or 任务状态 (Task Status) shows 未完成 (Not completed). Items showing "-", 已绑定 (Linked), 已完成 (Completed) or 手动解除 (Manually removed) no longer apply. You can also check 活动编号 (Promotion ID) and 中奖金额 (Prize Amount).
  2. 绑定和分享进度：查看同一条记录的「绑定状态」「分享进度」「任务状态」。
    Linking and share progress: In the same record, check 绑定状态 (Link Status), 分享进度 (Share Progress) and 任务状态 (Task Status).

问答 Q&A

1为什么我不能玩游戏，要绑定谷歌邮箱？Why do I have to link a Google email before playing?

回复玩家Reply
Congratulations on your big win! To keep your account secure, please link a Google email first by tapping "Continue with Google". If you don't have a Google account, tap "Create Google Account", sign up, then come back and tap "Continue with Google". You can play right after linking.
恭喜中大奖！为了账户安全，请先点「使用 Google 绑定」绑定谷歌邮箱。没有 Google 账号的，先点「注册 Google 账号」完成注册，回来再点「使用 Google 绑定」。绑定后即可开始游戏。
客服核查Check
分享用户记录里「绑定状态」是否为「未绑定」。
Check that 绑定状态 (Link Status) in Share Records shows 未绑定 (Not linked).
转交Escalate
绑定状态为「已绑定」但玩家仍被要求绑定时，转技术。
If Link Status shows linked but the player is still asked to link, escalate to Tech.

2提示「该邮箱已绑定其他账号」怎么办？It says "This email is linked to another account".

回复玩家Reply
Each Google account can only be linked to one player account. Please tap "Continue with Google" again and choose a Google account that has not been linked before.
一个 Google 账号只能绑定一个平台账号。请再点「使用 Google 绑定」，换一个没有绑定过的 Google 账号授权。
客服核查Check
无需核查。
No check needed.
转交Escalate
玩家坚持该邮箱是本人的，或疑似被他人冒用时，转运营。
If the player insists the email is theirs, or the email may have been used by someone else, escalate to Operations.

3为什么提款要先分享？Why do I need to share before withdrawing?

回复玩家Reply
Big spin winners need to complete a one-time share task before withdrawing: tap "Share" in the pop-up the number of times shown. Then tap "Submit" again to withdraw. You won't be asked again.
转盘大奖玩家提款前需完成一次分享任务：在弹窗里点「去分享」，按弹窗显示的次数完成分享。完成后再点「提交」即可提款，以后不再要求。
客服核查Check
分享用户记录里的「任务状态」和「分享进度」。
Check 任务状态 (Task Status) and 分享进度 (Share Progress) in Share Records.
转交Escalate
玩家强烈投诉，或无法使用 Facebook / Messenger 时，转运营评估是否手动解除。
If the player complains strongly or cannot use Facebook / Messenger, escalate to Operations to decide whether to remove the restriction manually.

4我分享了，进度没有增加？I shared, but the progress didn't go up.

回复玩家Reply
We only count each time you tap "Share" in the pop-up and Facebook or Messenger opens (in the app, or on its web page if the app is not installed). Shares made outside the pop-up, such as from the Invite Friends page, are not counted. Please tap "Share" in the pop-up again.
只计在弹窗里点「去分享」并成功打开 Facebook / Messenger 的次数（打开 App，或未安装 App 时打开 H5 页面）；在弹窗外自己分享（如邀请好友页）不计。请在弹窗里再点一次「去分享」。
客服核查Check
「分享进度」里对应渠道的次数是否增加。
Check whether the count for that channel in 分享进度 (Share Progress) went up.
转交Escalate
玩家能提供从弹窗「去分享」打开 Facebook / Messenger 的截图，但「分享进度」没有增加时，转技术。
If the player has a screenshot showing Facebook / Messenger opened from "Share" in the pop-up, but 分享进度 (Share Progress) did not go up, escalate to Tech.

5为什么提款时要我先绑定谷歌邮箱？Why do I have to link a Google email before withdrawing?

回复玩家Reply
For account security, big spin winners need to link a Google email first. Tap "Continue with Google" to link it, then tap "Submit" again to continue your withdrawal. If you signed up with Google, your account is already linked.
为了账户安全，转盘大奖玩家需先绑定谷歌邮箱。点「使用 Google 绑定」完成绑定后，再点「提交」继续提款。用 Google 登录注册的账号已视为已绑定。
客服核查Check
分享用户记录里的「绑定状态」。
Check 绑定状态 (Link Status) in Share Records.
转交Escalate
绑定状态为「已绑定」但玩家仍被要求绑定时，转技术。
If Link Status shows linked but the player is still asked to link, escalate to Tech.

6活动已经结束了，为什么提款还要分享？The promotion has ended. Why do I still need to share before withdrawing?

回复玩家Reply
The share task still applies after the promotion ends. Please tap "Share" in the pop-up the number of times shown. You only need to do this once.
活动结束后，分享任务仍然有效。请在弹窗里点「去分享」，按弹窗显示的次数完成分享。只需完成一次。
客服核查Check
分享用户记录里的「任务状态」和「分享进度」。
Check 任务状态 (Task Status) and 分享进度 (Share Progress) in Share Records.
转交Escalate
玩家强烈投诉时，转运营评估是否手动解除。
If the player complains strongly, escalate to Operations to decide whether to remove the restriction manually.
