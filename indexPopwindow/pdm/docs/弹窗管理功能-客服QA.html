适用范围 Scope

789Bingo、789Games、789Play 的 H5、iOS、Android 首页（Home）弹窗（三个站各自在本站后台配置，互不影响）：玩家进入首页时弹出的合规弹窗、活动弹窗、公告，以及首页顶部的邀请好友提示条。充值 / 提款失败、实名认证结果、奖励到账、VIP 升级等由玩家操作或系统事件触发的弹窗，可能出现在任何页面，按各自的活动或系统逻辑弹出；它们出现在首页时，会等合规弹窗和当前弹窗关闭后再弹，不会叠在一起。从本需求上线起适用（上线时间以运营通知为准）。PC 端不在范围内。
Home page pop-ups on 789Bingo, 789Games and 789Play (H5, iOS, Android; each site is set up separately in its own back office): compliance pop-ups, promotion pop-ups and announcements shown when a player enters Home, plus the invite-friends bar at the top of Home. Pop-ups triggered by a player action or system event (deposit / withdrawal failure, KYC result, reward credited, VIP upgrade, etc.) can appear on any page and follow their own event or system logic; when they appear on Home, they wait until compliance pop-ups and the current pop-up are closed and never stack. Applies from the launch of this feature (launch date as announced by Operations). PC is not included.

这次改了什么 What changed

运营可以在后台调整首页弹窗的先后顺序、每个弹窗是否开启、游客和已登录玩家分别是否弹出和多久弹一次（每次回到首页都弹、每次打开或登录后弹一次、每天一次等）、一次最多弹几个，以及（在各活动里）弹窗按钮的文字和点击后去哪里。上线当天玩家看到的弹窗和现网一致，只有以下几处不同：① 789Bingo 旧版砸金蛋不再排到 21+ 等合规弹窗前面；② 从广告链接进入的游客也要先确认 21+；③ 789Games 先确认 21+、再确认协议；④ 21+ 确认按钮的英文统一为「I'm 21+」（现网已是这句的没有变化）；⑤ 处于自我排除、冷静期、禁止投注、冻结等负责任博彩状态的会员，不再看到活动、奖励和引导类弹窗和首页顶部邀请提示条；合规弹窗、登录公告（不带跳转按钮），以及奖励到账、结果通知和充值 / 提款失败、实名认证结果等账户通知照常；⑥「开启通知」引导弹窗改为按账号计次，同一账号换新手机登录不会再弹。789Play 上线时哪几处有变化，以运营通知为准。没升级到新版本的旧版 App 仍按原来的方式弹窗，以上变化对它不生效。已登录玩家在部分活动、活动图、公告弹窗上可能看到「当日免打扰」勾选，勾选后该弹窗当天不再弹，次日 00:00（GMT+8）恢复；未登录的游客没有这个勾选。公告可能带一个跳转按钮。
Operations can now adjust, in the back office, the order of Home pop-ups, whether each pop-up is on, whether and how often it shows for guests and for logged-in players (every time Home is opened, once after each app open or login, once a day, etc.), how many per visit, and each button's text and destination. On launch day players see the same pop-ups as before, with these differences: (1) on 789Bingo, the old egg-smash pop-up (旧版砸金蛋) no longer appears before the 21+ and other compliance pop-ups; (2) guests coming from ad links must also confirm 21+; (3) on 789Games, 21+ is confirmed before the agreement; (4) the 21+ confirmation button reads "I'm 21+" on all three sites (no change where it already does); (5) members under a responsible gaming status (self-exclusion, cooling-off, betting ban, freeze, etc.) no longer see promotion, reward and guide pop-ups or the invite bar at the top of Home; compliance pop-ups, login announcements (without a button), reward-credited and result notices, and account notices such as deposit / withdrawal failure and KYC results still appear; (6) the "turn on notifications" guide pop-up is now counted per account, so it does not show again when the same account logs in on a new phone. Which of these apply to 789Play at launch follows the Operations notice. Older app versions that have not been updated keep the old pop-up behaviour, and these changes do not apply to them. Logged-in players may see a "Don't show again today" checkbox on some promotion, banner and announcement pop-ups: once ticked, that pop-up stays hidden for the rest of the day and returns at 00:00 (GMT+8). Guests (not logged in) don't have this checkbox. Announcements may have a button that opens a page.

客服核查步骤 How to check

  1. 确认站点、端和身份：玩家用的是 789Bingo、789Games 还是 789Play，H5 还是 App（App 版本号）、是否已登录。三个站的弹窗各自在本站后台配置，查配置时到对应站的后台查。App 版本低于本期版本的，不读后台配置，按旧规则弹窗（见问答 14）。
    Site, device and status: 789Bingo, 789Games or 789Play, H5 or App (App version), logged in or guest. Each site's pop-ups are set up in that site's own back office, so check the settings there. App versions older than this release don't read the back-office settings and keep the old pop-up behaviour (see Q&A 14).
  2. 查弹窗配置：登录该玩家所在站的管理后台，打开【活动配置 › 首页弹窗管理中心】，用关键字找到该弹窗，看「显示顺序」「状态」、玩家当时对应的「未登录状态」或「登录状态」（「✓ 展示 · 显示方式」或「不展示」）、「当日免打扰」，以及弹窗名称下是否标「活动已结束」；列表上方「全局设置」里看「单次上限」。需要看全部设置时点操作列的「查看配置」（账号有编辑权限时显示为「修改配置」，只看不改，点「取消」退出）。
    Pop-up settings: in the player's site back office, open 活动配置 › 首页弹窗管理中心 (Activity Config › Home Pop-up Center), search the pop-up by keyword and check 显示顺序 (display order), 状态 (on/off), 未登录状态 / 登录状态 (logged out / logged in: "✓ 展示 · mode" means shown with that frequency, 不展示 means not shown), 当日免打扰 (don't show again today), and whether the name is tagged 活动已结束 (event ended). Check 单次上限 (max per visit) under 全局设置 (global settings) above the list. Click 查看配置 (view settings) in the action column for full details (shown as 修改配置 (edit settings) if your account can edit; only view, then click 取消 (cancel)).
  3. 查按钮去向：在首页弹窗管理中心点该弹窗的「来源活动」，进入活动配置页「弹窗按钮跳转」页签，在「事件触发信息」里找到玩家点的按钮，看「跳转位置」：写「不跳转，只关闭弹窗」表示运营配置的就是只关闭；抽奖、领取、砸蛋等按钮显示「触发逻辑」，点击后执行活动动作，之后按活动自身逻辑继续。要看页面是否停用或 App 是否支持，展开「系统页面清单」看「状态」和「最低 App 版本」。
    Button destination: in the Home Pop-up Center, click the pop-up's 来源活动 (source event) to open the event's 弹窗按钮跳转 (pop-up buttons) tab, find the button in 事件触发信息 (event triggers) and check 跳转位置 (destination). 不跳转，只关闭弹窗 means the button is set to only close the pop-up. Buttons such as spin, claim or smash show 触发逻辑 (what the button does) instead: they run the event action and then follow the event's own flow. To see whether a page is disabled or supported on App, open 系统页面清单 (system page list) and check 状态 (status) and 最低 App 版本 (minimum App version).
  4. 查活动资格：弹窗是否弹还取决于活动资格，在对应活动菜单查玩家是否满足条件、活动是否在进行中。
    Event eligibility: whether a pop-up shows also depends on eligibility. Check the event's own menu for the player's eligibility and whether the event is running.
  5. 查最近改动：在【日志管理 › 操作日志】看最近是否改过弹窗的顺序、状态、展示、显示方式，或按钮文案和链接（每次保存都记录修改人、时间和旧值 → 新值；保存后 5 分钟内生效）。
    Recent changes: 日志管理 › 操作日志 (Log Management › Operation Log) shows recent changes to pop-up order, on/off, shown / not shown, frequency, button text and links, with who, when and old → new values. Changes take effect within 5 minutes.
  6. 查会员状态：已登录玩家看不到任何活动弹窗时，先在会员资料里看是否处于自我排除、冷静期、禁止投注、冻结等负责任博彩状态；处于这些状态的会员不弹营销、奖励、引导类弹窗；合规弹窗、登录公告（不带按钮），以及到账、结果通知和账户通知照常，属于正常表现。
    Member status: if a logged-in player sees no promotion pop-ups at all, first check the member profile for a responsible gaming status (self-exclusion, cooling-off, betting ban, freeze, etc.). Such members don't get promotion, reward or guide pop-ups; compliance pop-ups, login announcements (without a button), reward-credited and result notices and account notices still appear; this is expected.

问答 Q&A

1我之前每次都能看到的活动弹窗，今天怎么没有了？The promo pop-up I used to see every time is gone today. Why?

回复玩家Reply
Hi! Pop-ups on the Home page are shown based on the event period, your eligibility and how often each one is set to appear. You may have already seen it today, chosen to hide it for today, or the event may have ended. You can still find running events on the Promotions page.
您好！首页弹窗会按活动时间、您的参与资格和弹出次数设置来显示。可能今天已经看过、勾选了「当日免打扰」，或活动已经结束。进行中的活动仍可以在活动页面找到。
客服核查Check
按核查步骤 2、4 查该弹窗的状态、在玩家当时状态（未登录 / 登录）下是否展示和显示方式、是否标「活动已结束」，以及玩家资格。状态关闭或该状态「不展示」只停首页自动弹出，活动页入口照常；显示方式为「首次打开」的，回到首页或 H5 同一标签页刷新都不再弹；单次上限已满时，排在后面的弹窗本次不弹；已登录玩家勾过「当日免打扰」的当天不再弹。以上都属正常。
Follow steps 2 and 4: the pop-up's on/off, whether it is shown and how often for the player's state (logged out / logged in), whether it is tagged as ended, and eligibility. Off or "not shown" only stops it on Home; the event page still works. With "first open", it does not show again when the player returns to Home or refreshes the same H5 tab. When the max per visit is reached, pop-ups further down the order wait for the next visit. A logged-in player who ticked "Don't show again today" won't see it that day. All of these are expected.
转交Escalate
配置和资格都满足但仍不弹，转技术（附站点、端、App 版本、发生时间）。
If settings and eligibility are fine but it still doesn't show, escalate to Tech (site, device, App version, time).

您好！未登录时，首页活动弹窗按各活动的设置显示，部分弹窗每次打开网站或 App 都会出现。「当日免打扰」需要登录后使用：在支持的弹窗上勾选后，当天不再显示。您也可以随时点 × 关闭弹窗。
客服核查Check

If a player asks to stop a pop-up just for them: CS can't do that; suggest logging in and using "Don't show again today", no escalation. If many players complain about the same pop-up, pass to Operations to review its frequency.
