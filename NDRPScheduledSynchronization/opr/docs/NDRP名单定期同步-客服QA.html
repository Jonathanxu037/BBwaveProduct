适用范围 Scope

789Bingo、789Games、789Play 三站的 KYC，含 H5、PC、iOS、Android；从本需求上线起。
KYC on 789Bingo, 789Games and 789Play (H5, PC, iOS, Android), from the release of this update.

这次改了什么 What changed

KYC 第一步改为先填写姓名。姓名与证件不一致的，提示「姓名不一致」，可修改后重新提交；未通过名单筛选的，提示「验证失败，请联系客服」。
KYC now starts with entering the name. If the name doesn't match the ID, the player sees "Name Mismatch" and can resubmit. If the player fails the screening, they see "Verification failed. Please contact support."

※ 不论中文还是英文，都不能向玩家提到 NDRP、名单或政府官员，也不说明玩家是否在名单内。

客服核查步骤 How to check

  1. 看不通过原因：【用户管理 › KYC审核】「KYC审核记录」，按账号查看原因（备注）。
    Check the reason: 用户管理 › KYC审核 › KYC审核记录 (User Management › KYC Review › KYC Review Records), look at the reason (remark) for the account.

问答 Q&A

1为什么我的验证失败了？Why did my verification fail?

回复玩家Reply
Your verification was not approved. We can forward your appeal to our compliance team.
验证未通过，可为您转交合规团队申诉。
客服核查Check
原因（备注）以「命中 NDRP 名单」开头的，不向玩家说明原因。
If the reason starts with "命中 NDRP 名单" (matched NDRP list), do not explain the reason.
转交Escalate
玩家申诉的（含说自己不在名单上的），转合规负责人核实。
If the player appeals (including saying they are not on any list), escalate to the compliance lead.

2提示姓名不一致怎么办？It says my name doesn't match. What should I do?

回复玩家Reply
Please enter your name exactly as shown on your ID and resubmit.
请按证件上的姓名填写，然后重新提交。
客服核查Check
原因（备注）为「证件姓名与填写姓名不一致」。
The reason shows "证件姓名与填写姓名不一致" (ID name does not match the entered name).
转交Escalate
按证件填写后仍提示不一致的：见下文「资料未覆盖的问题」。
If it still fails after entering the name as on the ID: see "Not covered yet" below.
