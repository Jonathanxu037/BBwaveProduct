Member withdrawals on all channels (H5, PC, iOS, Android). Launch date to be scheduled.

这次改了什么 What changed

平台增加了提现风控检查。会员提交提现时，如果命中任意一条检查规则，这笔提现会转入人工审核，会员端订单显示 Under review，审核完成后通过出款或驳回。会员界面和提交方式没有变化；新规则命中时不弹窗，原有的单笔、笔数、累计金额限额命中时照常弹窗。对会员不说明具体规则名称和阈值。
The platform has added risk checks on withdrawals. If a withdrawal matches any check, it goes to manual review and shows Under review until it is approved for payout or rejected. The member interface and how to submit are unchanged. No popup is shown for the new checks; the existing single-amount, daily-count and daily-total limits still show their popup. Do not tell members the specific rule names or thresholds.

客服核查步骤 How to check

  1. 订单是否在风控审核：【提现管理 › 提现风控审核】按会员名搜索，审核状态选「待审核」。
    Is the order under risk review: 提现管理 › 提现风控审核 (Withdrawal Management › Withdrawal Risk Review), search by username with status 待审核 (Pending Review).
  2. 为什么进入审核：点「查看命中详情」看命中的规则和原因，只用于内部判断，不告诉会员。
    Why it is under review: click 查看命中详情 (View Trigger Details). For internal use only; do not share with the member.
  3. 审核结果：审核状态「已通过」看【财务管理 › 提款记录】出款进度；「已驳回」看驳回原因，金额已退回余额。
    Review result: if 已通过 (Approved), check payout progress in 财务管理 › 提款记录 (Finance › Withdrawal Records); if 已驳回 (Rejected), check the reason — the amount has been returned to the balance.

问答 Q&A

1我的提现为什么一直显示 Under review？Why does my withdrawal keep showing Under review?

回复玩家Reply
Your withdrawal is being reviewed by our team as part of our routine security checks. Once the review is complete, it will be processed right away. Thank you for your patience.
您的提现正在按平台例行安全检查进行人工审核，审核完成后会立即处理，感谢耐心等待。
客服核查Check
在提现风控审核确认订单为「待审核」；不说明命中了哪条规则。
Confirm the order is Pending Review in Withdrawal Risk Review; do not say which rule was triggered.
转交Escalate
等待超过 30 分钟（后台显示风控超时）时转风控审核人员催办。
If it has waited more than 30 minutes (shown as risk review overdue), escalate to the risk review team.

2为什么我的提现要审核，别人的不用？Why does my withdrawal need review when others don't?

回复玩家Reply
Withdrawals are checked automatically for account and fund security, and some are selected for a manual review. This does not mean there is a problem with your account. We will process it as soon as the review is done.
为保障账户和资金安全，系统会自动检查提现，部分提现会转人工审核，并不代表账户有问题，审核完成后会尽快处理。
客服核查Check
不比较其他会员，不透露规则和阈值。
Do not compare with other members or reveal rules and thresholds.
转交Escalate
会员投诉或要求说明原因时转风控审核主管。
If the member complains or insists on a reason, escalate to the risk review supervisor.

3我的提现被驳回了，钱去哪了？My withdrawal was rejected. Where is my money?

回复玩家Reply
The amount of the rejected withdrawal has been returned to your account balance. Please check the reason in your inbox message. You may submit a new withdrawal request.
被驳回的提现金额已退回您的账户余额，原因请查看站内信，您可以重新申请提现。
客服核查Check
在提现风控审核查看驳回原因，在资金明细确认已退回余额。
Check the rejection reason in Withdrawal Risk Review and confirm the refund in Funds Details.
转交Escalate
资金明细里没有退回记录时转技术。
If no refund appears in Funds Details, escalate to tech.
